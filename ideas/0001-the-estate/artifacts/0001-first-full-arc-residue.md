# First full arc — residue

Raw material from the Steward's session on idea-0003 (the Starvu agency site),
T, 2026-08-27. Idea-0003 just ran the estate's first end-to-end test — capture
→ research ×5 → frame → challenge → envision → explore → chart → phase → seed —
closing at `ideas/0003-starvu-agency-site/state/0012.md` with the export at
`exports/0003-starvu-agency-site-seed.md`. T asked for the residue to be filed:
the observations about how the estate itself functioned, which do not belong on
idea-0003. Captured as they arrived.

1. **The Steward substituted a dispatch target.** T dispatched `challenge` at
   "the Horizon" — but no Horizon existed; the phrase T remembered
   ("specificity substitutes for proof") was nowhere on the record. The Steward
   redirected the attack to the Framing (the artifact the Horizon would have
   been built from) on its own judgment, stated the substitution openly, and it
   paid off. No rule covers when a dispatch names a target that does not exist.

2. **Conversation is a channel that bypasses artifacts, and it carried load.**
   T corrected the record twice in chat (the LLC exists; the flyer is entirely
   true — reversing an earlier misstatement), stated the strategy ("flashy,
   very VIP, as many meaningful features as possible") in chat, and delivered
   the careers tab as an existing fact in chat. All of it reached the artifacts
   only because dispatch packets quoted it. The v1 design prompt left the
   estate through chat entirely — an export with no artifact, no record, no
   provenance.

3. **The careers tab never went through `capture`.** It is now worked into
   seven Phase artifacts and the Seed, marked "arriving from T, not through
   capture" — a fact load-bearing in eight documents with no artifact of its
   own. The marking discipline held, but the seam exists.

4. **Non-canonical verb order paid off.** `challenge` ran before `envision`, so
   the claim that would have been the Horizon's spine was replaced before
   anything was built on it. The pipeline permits this; nothing encourages it.
   The counterfactual — envision on unchallenged ground, then attack — would
   have cost a full rewrite.

5. **The relay structure generated real criticism.** Every agent found
   something its predecessor "did not know about itself": the Advocate broke
   the Gardener's central claim; the Forager overturned the Architect's portal
   refusal by the Architect's own test; the Surveyor's phase pass corrected his
   own chart's ordering (the notes-capture fuse was already burning); the Sower
   caught that `challenged: false` on the Seed flattens which layers were
   attacked (the ground survived an adversarial pass; the Horizon, Trajectory
   and Phases never faced one). Disagreement between artifacts (`0010` vs
   `0011` on the portal) was logged as a live tension rather than silently
   resolved — that discipline held.

6. **Gold nuggets are promoted on phrasing alone.** The Advocate observed that
   state 0006 had promoted claims to "gold nuggets" on no evidence but their
   quotability — two of them contradicted each other and nobody noticed until
   the challenge. The Steward writes state and picks the nuggets; there is no
   check on the picking.

7. **Asset binaries have no route into the estate.** The logo and flyer
   arrived as conversation attachments on day one, are catalogued in
   `MANIFEST.md`, and after twelve states have still never been placed on
   disk. The Seed ships with all three asset rows reading `pending`.
   Attachments arrive in the channel that bypasses artifacts (item 2) and
   nothing converts them.

8. **`appetite: 1` survived twelve states as a placeholder.** Flagged in every
   state's open questions, never set. A hand-set field the operator never
   hand-sets is a field the system carries as noise.

9. **One session ran six verbs.** challenge, envision, explore, chart, phase,
   seed — plus two corrections and a pivot — in a single session with one
   state snapshot per verb. The close protocol assumes a session ends; this
   one accumulated. Nothing broke, but state-per-verb inside one session
   versus state-per-session is undefined.
