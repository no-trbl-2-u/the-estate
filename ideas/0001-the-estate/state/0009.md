# State 0009

## Established

The Gardener framed the challenged Spark — its own Spark, two days later and one

### The ground

> **The estate's provenance machinery — typing, lineage, classifiers,
> immutability, verb-addressability — reaches only what verbs produce, and the
> acts that steer the verbs are not produced by verbs.**

Short name: **the unaddressable steering layer.** The Gardener deliberately
refused to compress it into an aphorism.

**It is derivable from the design documents alone, with no reference to how the
first arc went.** The chain: `templates/artifact.md` requires `produced-by:`, so
an artifact is typed by the verb that made it; `system/registry.md` lists no verb
producing a dispatch packet, a route derivation, or a nugget selection — so **a
packet cannot be an artifact at all, by construction rather than by oversight.**
The writer seam then leaves state as its only legal home, and `challenge` is
`a → a`, so attacking a state would have to produce a state, which the seam
forbids.

> **Even the fully compliant remedy — the Steward writing packets into state —
> would not make them attackable. The unaddressability reproduces itself in an
> arc run perfectly to spec.**

**ADR 0013 decision 2 priced part of this at design time** (*"a poorly written
narrative degrades the record silently"*). It priced **narrative quality**. It
did not price **addressability**. That unpriced half is this Framing's
contribution.

### Why the other two lost

  its wall at the perimeter and counts only inbound traffic, while addition A
  establishes that **the largest unrecorded channel is internal.** It is the
  inbound half of the chosen ground; adopting it would guarantee remedying the
  smaller half — **the estate's own caution about visitors and staff, landing on
  the estate.**
- **Candidate 2 (the unexamined hub) loses narrowly, on diagnosis rather than
  subject.** Stated as an *asymmetry of attention* it implies negligence and
  invites a habit-shaped remedy — but **nobody could have aimed a verb at the
  hub**: no verb has it in its domain and none has a codomain the result could
  land in. Decisively: the attention framing **depends on how the first arc
  went, and this record's only account of that is broken** and the Gardener was
  forbidden to repair it. It is not rejected; it is **explained as the chosen
  ground's internal half, with its cause named.**
- **Candidate 3 (classifier consequence) is parked, not absorbed** — and its
  evidence points the other way. **Seed §10 exists *because* the Sower read the
