# State 0001

## Established

An outside audit was run against `AUDIT-PROMPT.md` and reported fifteen
confirmed defects and two suspicions (`AUDIT-REPORT.md`). All were resolved —
the front-door Spark hole, The Advocate's missing Write tool, the standing
identity's dependence on `AGENTS.md` loading, the writer seam's silence about
`idea.md`, the unmaintained `state-head`, the reachability formula that scored
raw Sparks at zero, and the rest — recorded in ADR 0015. Nothing was added to
the taxonomy: merge and split turned out to be compositions, and became
playbooks.

The project was then renamed **think-tank → The Estate** (repo `The-Estate`),
recorded in ADR 0016. The theme has been The Estate since ADR 0013; the
project name has now caught up with it.

## Decisions

- **All seventeen audit findings fixed rather than triaged.** The five that
  blocked real use (F-1, F-2, F-3, F-5, and the merge recipe) were the point;
  the rest were cheap once the files were open.
- **The historical record stays unedited through the rename.** `BRAINSTORM.md`,
  `BUILD-PROMPT.md`, the ADR set, and `AUDIT-REPORT.md` keep every "think-tank"
  in their bodies. Their banners note the rename instead. Rewriting them would
  falsify what was decided under the old name, and lineage is never discarded.
- **`state/0000.md` untouched.** It carries the pre-reconciliation
  `session-skill:` field that the audit flagged (F-14). It is immutable; the
  discrepancy is documented, not corrected.
- **The Dreamer is not built.** Its capability exists as the `split` playbook's
  composition, and each child costs an explicit operator decision — a stronger
  guard than any cap. Revisit as `germinate` (The Gardener, `Framing → [Spark]`,
  children born `incubating`, cap 3) only after the falsifier passes *and* the
  manual composition has been performed by hand three or more times.
