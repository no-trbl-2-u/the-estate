# State 0001

## Established

The estate ran its first full arc on a real idea: idea-0002 went from a
`/start` prompt to a sealed layout Decision in one session — ten
artifacts, nine snapshots, seven verbs (`capture`, `research`,
`explore` ×3, `frame`, `challenge`, `envision`, `chart`, `decide`), two
dispatches, six pull requests. A second `SessionResidue` records nine
observations from it, eight of them critical.

The headline items: the design round that produced the session's
largest body of work happened **outside the estate**, because nothing
here can hold a set of candidates; the assumption that cost a full
rebuild — that the record sheet was the interface — was never written
in any artifact, and a `fresh-eyes` challenge did not surface it; and
the delta close is producing snapshots that are mostly copies, by
following its own rule.

## Decisions

- **The residue is filed on idea-0001, not idea-0002**, per
  `system/TYPES.md`: the observations are about the machinery, not
  about Rivers and Lakes. Idea-0002's own states carry its content.
- **Item 9 is included even though it is not about the machinery.**
  A residue that inventories nine defects while the project's own
  falsifier runs out has failed at proportion; the deadline is named
  in the artifact and here.
- **Nothing was fixed in this session.** Unlike the first residue,
  which repaired two defects as it found them, every item here is
  reported and left. Three of them (the missing candidate type, the
  `explore`-as-catch-all question, the delta close contradiction) are
  law-shaped and belong to T and an ADR, not to a session that noticed
  them.

## Gold nuggets

> move the estate has no verb for.

> that nobody has asked for.

## Tensions

- **Machinery against use, with a deadline.** Carried from state 0000
  and sharpened: the estate now has a Seed-shaped record and still no
  export. 2026-09-25, twenty days out. Only `seed` and T's word settle
  it.
- **The delta close against its own rule.** Carrying the live sets in
  full is required and produces snapshots that are mostly duplicates.
  Both readings are legitimate; neither has been chosen.
- **Verb economy against verb fit.** An inline refiner is cheap and a
  boundary verb needs T's word, so the cheap one absorbs work the
  expensive one was written for. Whether this is drift or the design
  working is undecided.

## Open questions

1. **Does the estate need a way to hold candidates?** A type, a shape,
   or nothing. `compare` exists but operates on artifacts, and options
   built outside never become artifacts.
2. **Should `inputs:` paths be checked for resolution?** Mechanical,
   cheap, and the estate's own law says a claim the validator does not
   check is guidance, not law.
3. **Is one record's arc one branch?** Six PRs for one session is what
   the current rules produce.
4. **Should the performing voice be bound to the operator's
   vocabulary**, as The Gardener is in `capture` and `jot`, wherever it
   converses?
5. **Is there a verb for showing the operator a thing rather than
   describing it?** The one move that found the session's largest
   defect has no name here.
6. Carried from state 0000, unanswered: does anything check that the
   tools reading the estate still work; is there a mechanism for
   guidance whose premise expires.
