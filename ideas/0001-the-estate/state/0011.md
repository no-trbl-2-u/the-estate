# State 0011

## Established

**The Chancellor's office was called for the first time in this estate's
history.** Every snapshot on every record until now has recorded that it never
had; idea-0003 exported a Seed carrying four open decisions past a silent
Chancellor. T stated a standing convention for all roadmaps, and because that
is a decision rather than a preference, the verb was dispatched rather than
absorbed into a Steward structural session.

The Decision (`0005-human-attention-tag.md`): every step in every roadmap —
Trajectory legs and Phase steps alike — is evaluated for whether a human must
perform it. Steps an agent **definitely cannot perform** carry
`**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**`; steps an agent can perform carry nothing, and that
silence is an assertion rather than a default.

### The distinction the decision turns on

**Needing T's approval is not needing T's hands.** This estate already reserves
all authority to T, so a tag keyed to approval would mark every step and
discriminate nothing. The tag marks work an agent cannot do *however freely
approval is granted* — purchases, credentials and consent, physical acts, acts
inside T's own relationships, taste T has reserved, choices whose content is
T's judgment, anything a human must be answerable for. The Chancellor recorded
it as a procedure a future Surveyor can actually run: grant that approval is
already given, then ask whether an agent can still carry the step to done.

The office also declined a convenience the Steward's packet had left open: it
would not cite idea-0004's roadmap as an `input:`, because that would draw a
cross-record edge sideways, and only `relate` may author one. `inputs: []` with
the boundary reasoning in-body. The seam held under its own first test.

### Where the convention now lives

Recorded in the record, but **binding through the skills**: both
`.claude/skills/chart/SKILL.md` and `.claude/skills/phase/SKILL.md` carry it,
because a spawned agent reads neither `AGENTS.md` nor an ADR
(`system/LAW.md`, *Where the law lives*). A convention recorded only in an idea
record would have been invisible to exactly the population it binds. Logged as
[ADR 0020](../../../docs/adr/0020-human-attention-tag.md); the skill and ADR
edits are the Steward's steering, and appear in this snapshot's outputs as
paths.

## Tensions

- The tag is a claim about **agent capability**, and capability moves. The
  Chancellor's reading, worth carrying: a capability change should prune the
  *capability* categories and never the *reserved* ones — T's chosen limits do
  not move when agents get better.
