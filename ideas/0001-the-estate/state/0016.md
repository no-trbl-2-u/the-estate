# State 0016

## Established

The six load-bearing structural changes decided in the 2026-08-31 architecture
review were **implemented in full**, as five ADRs plus a sixth for the residue
ruling T made this session. No verb ran: this was a Steward structural session,
as ADRs 0015–0020 were before it.

**1. The audience seam (ADR 0021).** Verbs now carry a **mode**. Four —
`frame`, `challenge`, `decide`, `explore` — run as an **audience**: the Steward
dispatches, introduces the operator, and steps out; the operator converses with
the bound agent directly. The **handback packet** is defined once in
`system/LAW.md` (artifact path, classifier verdicts, gold nuggets, open
questions, tensions) and the transcript is never duplicated into the record.
The dialogue relay is gone; a batch agent that needs an answer now stays alive
for it instead of being re-dispatched.

**2. Proposal-only dispatch (ADR 0022).** The Steward proposes the verb and
**waits**. A described intent is never an invocation, however obvious the
derivation. The degrade clause survives, narrowed to its one case: an operator
who *names* the verb has already selected it.

**3. `jot` and the inbox (ADR 0023).** A stray thought lands in
`inbox/YYYYMMDD-HHMM-slug.md` in one step — verbatim, one-line confirmation, no
record, no artifact, no state, no close. Performed by the Steward as a
**clerical duty**, not a bound verb, because a `Slip` is a boundary input and
no artifact is produced. Slips are stamped when processed, never deleted.

**4. `graft` (ADR 0024).** Branching, which `README.md` has promised since the
first build and no verb implemented. A new record from any prior snapshot,
inheriting that snapshot's tips, carrying a required **Direction**, with
lineage written in both directions. Bound to **The Gardener** per D1.

**5. Seed payload and nameable contracts (ADR 0025).** The five components
become the default `standard` contract; domain contracts are nameable, with
three invariants (a next move, a refusal, the provenance stamp). A Seed may
carry a **payload directory** that *is* the droppable result. `payload:
present | absent` is a classifier, never a gate.

**6. `SessionResidue` (ADR 0026).** T ruled: bless the pattern. A
`capture`-produced type for what a session reveals about the estate's own
functioning.

## Decisions

**ADR numbering shifted to 0021–0026.** The plan specified 0020–0025; `0020` was
already taken by `0020-human-attention-tag.md`, decided in state 0015.

**`reference/` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` were updated inside each phase, not in the
final sweep.** The plan's sweep list omitted both. They mirror `system/`
verbatim, so leaving them stale between phases would have been the drift failure
the estate is built against — the one thing `system/` being a single source of
truth exists to prevent.

**The pre-existing hand-run branching procedure is superseded, on three of four
points.** Found during the sweep, not anticipated by the plan:
`ideas/README.md` and `reference/record-model.md` both documented "Branching,
exactly" — performed by the *Steward*, copying no artifacts, writing no
back-edge, leaving the source wholly untouched. That is four direct
contradictions with `graft`, and shipping both would have left two incompatible
answers to "how do I branch." The performer, the inherited tips, and the
back-edge resolve in `graft`'s favour. The **cross-record `previous:` pointer
resolves in the old procedure's favour and is kept** — it is a cleaner `parent`
derivation than Origin prose, and it is what makes ADR 0024's claim that graft
edges are *derivable* concretely true rather than merely arguable.

**`survey` teaching was not free, contrary to the plan's expectation.** Phase 4
step 6 said graft support should mostly fall out of `relates` already existing,
and said *verify, don't assume*. Verification found `survey` never displayed
`relates` edges at all. It now reports descendants and fork points, and
`system/SCORING.md` counts **authored edges only** toward `connective` — a
record branched from repeatedly must not drift toward `connective` for having
been used as a starting point.

**The existing seven residues are not retyped, and the two existing exports are
not amended.** Artifacts are immutable; that law does not bend for tidiness.
Both conventions apply from the next one forward.

## Gold nuggets

> A verb performed inline runs in a context where `AGENTS.md` is loaded; the
> same verb dispatched runs in a subagent where it is not.

The sentence that decided the audience seam's shape. It is *why* the Steward
cannot simply carry the conversation on an agent's behalf — the obvious fix is
the one thing the estate cannot do.

> A graft without a Direction is a copy.

> Nine times in ten it is the right verb, and the tenth is not a wasted turn but
> a durable one.

The asymmetry that makes proposal-only dispatch a rule rather than a courtesy:
in a repository where nothing is ever deleted, a wrong inference is not undone.

> The sessions that generate the richest residue are exactly the ones where no
> verb ran.

Why the handback packet could not fold `SessionResidue` — idea 0004's three
residues came from a build, a deploy, and a debugging investigation, with no
dispatch to produce a packet.

> A build session whose central work has no verb the estate knows.

From residue `0012`. Still true, and still unaddressed: this round did not add
a verb for it.

## Tensions

**The inbox can become a second graveyard.** `jot` removes the friction that
kept thoughts out of the estate, and nothing drains the inbox. The greeting
check (count and **ages**) is the only thing standing between a shelf of slips
and a pile — and it is a habit encoded in a skill, not a mechanism.

**`exports/` stops being uniformly readable.** Payload directories put built
artifacts in a repository that has so far held only records and documents, and
nothing prunes them. Consistent with the preservation law, and still a real
change in what this repository *is*.

**Audience mode is unexercised.** Every claim about it — that direct
conversation improves the four verbs, that agents hand back packets rather than
retellings, that the Steward can genuinely stay quiet — is a design assertion.
None has been run once.

**The named failure mode is live.** `system/FALSIFIERS.md`: *building it is more
fun than using it.* This session added two verbs, a type, a directory, and six
ADRs, and moved no idea toward a Seed. That is defensible only if the
verification below actually happens.

## Open questions

**Phase 6's live verification is not done.** Three exercises remain, and each
needs T and real ground — a synthetic run would be worse than none, since
nothing here is ever deleted:

- **(a) Audience** — `/challenge` against a real artifact. The plan's candidate
  is idea 0003's unchallenged Horizon `0011`, whose own state `0012` names this
  exact gap.
- **(b) Jot** — jot a real stray thought, confirm it surfaces at greeting, then
  process it end to end.
- **(c) Graft** — a graft T actually wants. Idea 0003 has thirteen snapshots to
  fork from. Explicitly **not** a synthetic test: a throwaway record would live
  in the estate forever.
- **(d) Payload** — waits for a record genuinely ready to export. Forcing one
  would be the named failure mode with extra steps.

**Does `capture` doing double duty hold?** `SessionResidue` was blessed without
its own verb (ADR 0026, scope). If residue keeps arriving, the question of
whether it deserves a verb reopens.

**The counterfactual study is still unrun** — estate-versus-plain-conversation.
The plan deliberately excludes it as a study rather than a structural change; it
belongs to this record's own route as a later `research` or `review`.

## Current state declaration

The estate's structure is now complete against the vision it has been carrying
since the first build: branching exists, intake is cheap, four verbs are
conversations, Seeds can carry the thing rather than only describe it, and the
Steward proposes rather than infers. Twenty skill directories, eighteen bound
verbs plus one clerical duty, eleven artifact types, thirteen offices — and
every system file, the `reference/` bundle, and the glossary agree with each
other, verified by grep rather than asserted.

What it does **not** have is a single run of any of it. Every mechanism added
today is unexercised, and the honest reading is that this session is exactly the
shape of the failure mode `system/FALSIFIERS.md` names: elaborating the
machinery is more fun than using it. The project falsifier's check date is
**2026-09-25**, twenty-five days out, and it asks for evidence of use *outside*
this repo — which no amount of further structure can supply.

The next session on this record should be verification, not design.
