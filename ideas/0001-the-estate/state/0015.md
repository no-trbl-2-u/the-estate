# State 0015

## Established

T reviewed the estate's first Decision and corrected its **emphasis, not its
rule**. The Chancellor amended `0008-human-attention-tag.md` in place before
merge; the skills and ADR 0020 were re-cut to match.

What changed: the criterion now leads alone, in T's own words — *can an agent
do this step? If yes, no tag; if it is a "no matter what, AI can't do this,"
it gets the tag.* What did not change: the rule itself, the qualifying
categories, the grey-band rule, the reopening conditions, and every tag
already applied to idea-0004's roadmap.

### The defect worth naming

The first draft introduced the rejection of "needs T's approval" as *the
load-bearing distinction, and the reason the tag carries information at all.*
That rejection is real and is kept — a tag keyed to approval would mark every
step in an estate that approves everything by law, and discriminate nothing.
But it is a **guard against drift, not the definition**, and stating it first
made a rejected alternative read as part of the criterion. T read exactly that
and pushed back.

**The guard had grown larger than the thing it guarded.** A decision whose
framing obscures its own rule is a defect this office should watch for, and it
was caught by the operator rather than by the office — worth recording plainly
on the estate's own record, since this is the first Decision the estate has
ever held and its first correction arrived the same day.

reached `main` first (PR #19: this record's artifacts `0005`–`0007` and states
`0011`–`0013`; idea-0004's artifact `0014` and state `0008`). On merge, this
session's work was renumbered to follow it — the Decision from `0005` to
`0008`, these two snapshots from `0011`/`0012` to `0014`/`0015`, and on
idea-0004 the roadmap from `0014` to `0015` with its snapshots from
`0008`/`0009` to `0009`/`0010`. Every `inputs:`, `outputs:`, `previous:` and
in-body citation was rewritten to match, and the sequences are contiguous.

**Nothing was overwritten and nothing was dropped** — both sessions' artifacts
survive whole, which is what the estate's no-deletion rule requires. But the
numbers now record merge order rather than authorship order: the Decision
sealed before PR #19 landed sits at `0008`, after artifacts written later.
Recorded plainly because a reader deriving chronology from numbers on this
record would be wrong.
