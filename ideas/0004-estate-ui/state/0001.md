1. **Map first, tree deferred.** The lineage graph is entirely derivable today;

   not exist, and closing either gap is a record-schema change belonging to T

   `relates`-neighbors *as* the two honest meanings of "sub-idea," labeled with
   glossary terms. **Stated assumption: if T's real friction is
   grouping/nesting rather than seeing travel, this priority inverts.**
2. **Read-only viewer; dispatch is a far horizon, explicitly out of scope.**
   Two independent walls: any write path makes the UI **a third writer the law
   does not have** (a proposal to amend `system/LAW.md`, and must arrive as
   such), and a dispatch button originates exactly the steering acts idea-0001
   named unaddressable. The stated cost is carried openly: **the map shows
   every stop and none of the steering** — the Framing chooses to draw the
   addressable half and **label the gap visibly** rather than pretend
   completeness.
3. **In-repo static, opened locally.** Self-containment is law, not taste; the
   UI is *"a view, not a database,"* with `docs/idea-pipeline.html` as
   precedent. Build-time-generated versus runtime-parsed is left to
