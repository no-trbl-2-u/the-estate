travels, and clean navigation between projects and the ideas within them. The
Gardener captured the Spark with T's words verbatim in the Origin.

Two things preceded the capture in the same session, and the record inherits
both:

   **addressable** half of the estate. The steering layer (handoff packets,
   route derivations, nugget selection) produces no artifacts. **A map of how
   the idea travels cannot currently show what steered the travel.**
