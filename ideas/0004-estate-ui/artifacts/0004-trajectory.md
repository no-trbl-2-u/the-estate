# The route to the viewer

I am The Surveyor. Before I draw a single leg, the honest opening move the
Horizon assigned me:

**`appetite:` on this record is still the template placeholder `1`. Nobody has
set the heat.** Everything below is therefore sized to a guess — afternoons and
weekends, one operator-builder — and the route is built so that a low-appetite
answer from T *truncates* it instead of invalidating it. Every leg ends with
something openable; stopping after any leg leaves a working thing, not a
construction site. If T sets appetite low, the route is legs 1–2 and the watch.
If T sets it to zero, leg 1 alone is still a testable, keepable artifact: the
graph derivation the estate will want whenever this record wakes.

## The destination, restated in one line

One self-contained HTML file in the repo, regenerated by the command that opens
it, rendering the estate as travel: a portfolio shelf answering "where is
everything and what moved" in five seconds, and per-record maps drawing the
derived lineage against the Steward's state rail with the steering gap as a
labeled seam. Read-only, no dispatch surface, no dependency the repo cannot
vendor, every label through `docs/GLOSSARY.md`.

## The failure mode this route is shaped against

The named failure mode is a long polish tail before first use — a viewer that
accumulates artifacts while never being opened, inside the estate's own
2026-09-25 clock. So the route's governing rule: **every leg moves the viewer
toward being opened, and "T opens it on real data" happens at the end of leg
2** — not at the end of the route. The map, the panel, the search: all of it
lands *after* the viewer is already a thing T opens, so that from leg 3 onward
every session with the code is also a session of use.

## The legs

### Leg 1 — The parser and the proof (generator before pixels)

The Horizon's first ordering constraint, obeyed literally. A zero-dependency
generator script walks `ideas/`, `exports/`, and `ideas/SURVEY.md`, parses the
narrow frontmatter subset actually in use, and derives the whole graph:
version-chain stacking and tip-finding (same type + same-type `inputs:`
predecessor), fan-out detection, cross-boundary Seed edges, the single backward
provenance line from `origin:`, state↔artifact rungs from state `inputs:`/
`outputs:`, inbound-citation counts, tempo from state dates. Output: **plain
text**, verified against idea-0003's known facts — the 0008→0009 challenge
branch with 0009 as tip, the 0010→0011 chain caught by the same rule, the
seven-way fan-out from 0012, the Seed's nine inbound edges through the wall and
its one line pointing home to state/0011. No HTML exists at the end of this
leg, and the leg is still shippable: the derivation is the product's spine, and
it is testable in isolation exactly as the Horizon ordered.

**Dependency:** none. This is the first actionable step — startable this
Saturday.

### Leg 2 — The shelf and the open command (the surface falsifier 1 measures)

The Horizon's second ordering constraint. The generator grows its render step:
one self-contained HTML file with an embedded JSON data island, showing the
four cards — status, state-head recency, tempo strip, artifact/state counts,
the exported badge with its origin stamp, the appetite-unset pattern strip, the
inbound-citation numbers — plus the survey panel in its honest empty state and
the generated-at / `covers:` stamp always visible at the top. And the **open
command**: one invocation that regenerates and launches the browser, so
freshness collapses into opening. **This leg ends with T opening the viewer on
real data.** Falsifier 1's clock starts here, which is the point of putting it
second: the route reaches its own measurement surface in two legs.

**Dependency:** leg 1's derivation passing its fixture assertions.

### Leg 3 — The travel map (the thing T actually asked for)

The per-record view: state rail on the left with session-verbs, artifact field
on the right with the ten-type glyph vocabulary, version chains stacked
tip-bright, the fan-out fanned, the Seed outside the record's wall with the
backward provenance line drawn distinctly, the uniform hatched steering seam
between every pair of states carrying its full label, rungs tying rail to
field, the assets strip reading `MANIFEST.md` statuses. This is the biggest
leg, and it is third — not first — because everything it draws is a rendering
of derivations leg 1 already proved, and because by the time it lands the
viewer is already something T opens.

**Dependency:** leg 2 shipped and opened at least once. This leg also unlocks
the first possible reading of held falsifier 5 (does the seam read as honesty
or noise) — the reading is taken, not assumed.

### Leg 4 — The reading panel and the clean jump

The artifact panel (frontmatter header with the verb→agent chip resolved
through the registry, summary as standfirst, rendered body, tip-first chain
navigation with the superseded banner, OKF source tables with `stale_after:`
comparison) and the three properties of "cleanly": place-preserving back-stack,
head-oriented arrival, glossary-labeled traversable links — plus search over
titles and summaries with T's words as synonyms. This completes the Horizon's
day-one tier. It is deliberately the tail: if appetite cuts here, the shelf and
map still stand and artifact bodies are one file-open away, which is a
degradation, not a failure.

**Dependency:** leg 3's map, since the panel opens from its nodes.

### Leg 5 — The watch (not a build)

A month of steady use, read against the falsifiers where they actually sit:
falsifier 1 (five consecutive record-sessions orienting by files → **stop the
record, do not iterate**); falsifier 3 (the staleness banner on most opens →
take the pre-named local-server reversal, the most reversible refusal on the
Horizon's list); falsifier 5's accumulating reading (seam as noise → the
pressure travels to idea-0001 through the `relates` edge, never fixed here);
falsifier 4 armed but dormant until the first non-product record enters the
estate. The cheap unlocks land during this leg as *data acts, not code*: one
`survey` run fills the panel, one `relate` session draws the first edges, T
placing three binaries lights the assets strip, T setting `appetite:` anywhere
turns the unset-pattern strip into signal.

**Dependency:** a shipped viewer and elapsed time. Cost in build effort:
approximately zero, which is what makes it a leg and not an afterthought — the
route explicitly ends in reading, not in more building.

## Stations that span the route

Two falsifiers do not sit on a single leg:

- **The clock (falsifier 2).** 2026-09-25 is roughly four weekends away and
  crosses the entire route. Standing rule at every leg boundary: if the
  estate's governing falsifier has fired and this record is still accumulating
  artifacts, the route halts wherever it is. Only T reads this station.
- **Domain-generality (falsifier 4).** Cannot fire until a non-product record
  arrives; leg 3's glyph vocabulary is where it would bite, so the map is built
  from the domain-neutral type table and nothing else, and the station stays
  armed.

## What charting exposed that the Horizon did not know

The Horizon's shelf shows **inbound-citation counts** and cites its own Framing
as an arrow into idea-0001. But lineage is derived from `inputs:` frontmatter
alone (`system/TYPES.md`), and in the fixture set the *only* cross-record
frontmatter edges are the Seed's — idea-0004's artifacts cite only their own
record's files in `inputs:`; the pointing at idea-0001 lives in body prose.
The generator therefore computes inbound citations from `inputs:` only, and on
today's data idea-0001's badge honestly reads **zero**. Body-text mentions are
not edges, and a viewer that counted them would be hand-drawing lineage the
law says is derived. The empty badge joins the empty survey panel and the
empty `relates` layer as a third honestly-empty layer — filled the day
`relate` runs, not by fuzzy matching.

## Placement and stack — routine calls, made and stated as assumptions

- **Home:** `docs/estate-view/` — beside the `docs/idea-pipeline.html`
  precedent, as the state suggested. Generator at
  `docs/estate-view/generate.mjs`, output at `docs/estate-view/index.html`,
  committed like `SURVEY.md` (derived data lives in the repo; the repo stays
  the single source and the file stays deletable without loss).
- **Runtime:** Node.js, already ambient on a working software engineer's
  machine, **zero npm dependencies** — the frontmatter subset in use is narrow
  enough to hand-parse, and anything ever vendored goes under
  `docs/estate-view/vendor/`. If Node turns out not to be on T's machine, any
  single-file script runtime substitutes; the design does not care.
- **Open command:** a one-line repo script (`view.ps1` / `view.cmd` beside the
  generator) that runs the generator and opens the result — regeneration and
  opening as one act, per the Horizon's mitigation of its own falsifier 3.

None of these would make the work useless if reversed; all are reversible in
an afternoon.
