# Phase 5 — The watch

I am The Surveyor. The last Phase builds nothing, and that is its entire
point: the route ends in *reading*, because a route that only ever ends in
more building is the named failure mode with better manners. Molecularity
stops here — decomposing a month of use any further would be dust.

## What this Phase delivers

A verdict, gathered from stations already placed:

1. **Falsifier 1 — the unopened viewer.** The count is live from Phase 2's
   first open: five consecutive record-sessions in which T orients by opening
   files, or "what moved last week?" answered faster without the viewer.
   **Re-route: the record stops.** Not a redesign, not a feature — the Framing
   said stop, not iterate, and the watch holds it to that.
2. **Falsifier 3 — the staleness banner wins.** The evidence surface is the
   stamp Phase 2 put at the top of the shelf. If, after a month, the stale
   `covers:` banner is what most opens show, the build-time call was the wrong
   half of the deferred decision. **Re-route: the pre-named local-server
   reversal** — explicitly design, not law, the most reversible refusal on the
   Horizon's list — proposed to T as a small follow-on, not smuggled in.
3. **Falsifier 5 — the seam as noise.** Phase 3 took the first reading; the
   watch takes the accumulated one: does a month of sessions end in "but *why*
   did it go there?" **Re-route: idea-0001**, through the `relates` edge, once
   drawn. This record labels the gap; it never closes it.
4. **Falsifier 4 — armed, dormant.** Fires only when a non-product record
   enters the estate; the watch notes it as pending, and the first conjecture
   or premise captured becomes its test day.
5. **Falsifier 2 — the clock.** 2026-09-25 sits inside this Phase's window and
   spans every phase before it. Only T can read it. If the estate's governing
   falsifier fires while this record has grown fat, the verdict writes itself
   and the watch's job is only to not look away.

## The cheap unlocks — data acts, not code

Each lights a layer the earlier Phases already built, with no code change:

- One **`survey`** run (The Cartographer) fills the panel and its `covers:`.
- One **`relate`** session draws the first edges — the records themselves have
  already named the natural ones (0004↔0001, 0004↔0003-as-fixture).
- T places the **three Starvu binaries** the manifest catalogues as pending;
  the assets strip gains thumbnails.
- T sets **`appetite:`** anywhere; the unset-pattern strip becomes signal —
  and on this record most of all, since the whole route was sized to a
  placeholder.

None of these is this record's work to perform; the watch merely notices
whether the affordances built for them were built right when they land.

## Trigger, dependencies, cost

- **Trigger:** any shipped surface (the watch begins at Phase 2's first open
  and simply continues past Phase 4) plus elapsed time — call it a month of
  ordinary estate use.
- **Waits on:** real sessions happening for reasons other than this record.
  The watch cannot be manufactured; opening the viewer to admire it is not a
  session and does not count toward or against falsifier 1.
- **Cost:** approximately zero build effort; minutes of honest attention per
  session.

## Done looks like

One paragraph T can hand the Steward, closing the loop the Trajectory opened:
opened or unopened; fresh or stale; seam honest or noisy; clock survived or
fired. From that paragraph the record's next verb chooses itself — steady use
and nothing more to build, a reversal proposal, a pressure routed to
idea-0001, or a stop. Any of those is a clean ending; only an unread month is
a failure of this Phase.
