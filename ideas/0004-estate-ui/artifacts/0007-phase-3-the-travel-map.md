# Phase 3 — The travel map

I am The Surveyor. The biggest Phase on the route, placed third on purpose: by
the time it is built, the viewer is already something T opens, so every build
session is also a use session.

## What this Phase delivers

Clicking a shelf card opens the record as two parallel tracks — **the writer
seam, drawn**:

1. **The state rail.** Left edge, every state node dated with its
   `session-verb`, the head marked. The legend says this is the only column
   the Steward writes.
2. **The artifact field.** Every artifact a node, typed by glyph and color
   from the ten-type vocabulary — **taken verbatim from `system/TYPES.md`'s
   table and from nowhere else**, because falsifier 4 (the first conjecture
   must render as well as Starvu) is armed in exactly this choice. Edges are
   `inputs:` chains only.
3. **The three signature structures**, rendered from Phase 1's derivations
   with no special-casing: version chains stacked (0009 bright and marked tip
   with its `challenged` scar-glyph, 0008 dimmed and marked superseded; the
   same rule catching 0010→0011); the fan-out (0012 opening into seven Phase
   nodes in phase order); the boundary (the record's right border a wall, the
   Seed outside it, nine inbound edges through the wall, and the **one
   backward line** from the Seed to state/0011 rendered unlike every other
   edge — the map teaching that only one thing ever travels home).
4. **The rungs.** Where a state carries `inputs:`/`outputs:`, thin lines tie
   rail to field: consumed on one side, produced on the other.
5. **The seam.** Between every pair of states, a uniform hatched band; hover
   yields the full label the Horizon wrote — every stop, none of the steering,
   the gap named in idea-0001, not closed here. Uniform because the record
   genuinely cannot distinguish one gap from another; uniformity **is** the
   honest rendering.
6. **The assets strip.** `MANIFEST.md` rows with statuses — on the fixture,
   three entries, all pending, no thumbnails until binaries exist.

**Done looks like:** the Starvu map shows the branch, the fan-out, and the
wall at a glance; idea-0001's map (three artifacts, ten states) and
idea-0002's near-empty map render from the same code without a single
record-specific line; and this record's own map shows its fresh chain,
Spark → Framing → Horizon → Trajectory → five Phases fanning out.

## Trigger, dependencies, cost

- **Trigger:** Phase 2 shipped and opened at least once by T.
- **Waits on:** nothing else. Layout is hand-written SVG or positioned HTML in
  the emitter; if a layout library ever earns its way in, it is vendored under
  `docs/estate-view/vendor/` — no dependency the repo cannot carry.
- **Cost:** two weekends, split honestly. **Weekend one:** correct topology,
  ugly — rail, field, edges, chains, fan-out, wall, all placed right.
  **Weekend two:** glyphs, seam bands, rungs, assets strip, legibility. If
  weekend two starts stretching, ship the ugly-correct map and stop:
  correctness is the product, prettiness is the polish tail wearing a
  roadmap.

## The checkpoint: falsifier 5's first reading

The held falsifier — *the steering gap dominates the view* — becomes readable
for the first time the day this Phase ships, and the Phase closes by taking
the reading rather than assuming it: does the hatched seam read as honesty or
as noise when T actually looks at the Starvu map? **Re-route on noise:** the
pressure travels to idea-0001 via the `relates` edge The Cartographer has not
yet drawn — it is never answered by synthesizing steering content here, which
the Horizon refuses with the wall named (the data does not exist). A visual
quieting of the band is permitted; an informational filling of it is not.

## Where a Framing assumption bites

Assumption 1 (map before tree) is spent here — this Phase is that bet at full
price. If, with the map in hand, T's friction is still "I can't group and
nest," the answer is the structural proposal to T with the reading list, not
another rendering pass. Assumption 4 bites again at render time: any
unresolvable `inputs:` link draws as a visible broken edge, labeled as such.

## What this Phase refuses

Steering-content synthesis, any "why did it go there" narration, a Project
tier, child records, and per-domain glyph flourishes. The map draws
frontmatter and only frontmatter.
