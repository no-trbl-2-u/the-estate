## Tensions

<!-- Unresolved conflicts and COMPETING FRAMINGS — two live readings pulling
     against each other. Distinct from open questions, which await an answer;
     a tension awaits a choice, and may never be resolved at all. -->

- **The map T wants and the map the record can draw are not the same map.**
  T asks to "see the map of how the idea is traveling." idea-0001's Framing
  (`ideas/0001-the-estate/artifacts/0003-framing.md`) established that the
  lineage a UI would draw covers only the *addressable* half of the estate —
  the steering layer (handoff packets, route derivations) produces no
  artifacts. A map of how the idea travels cannot currently show what steered
  the travel. Either the UI draws the addressable half and says so, or the
  estate starts making the steering layer addressable — a structural change,
  T's call. Not yet chosen.
