# The operator-supplied material seam

## Origin

<!-- The prompt or context that initiated this idea, verbatim. Never edited. -->

T, 2026-08-27, closing the ADR 0019 session:

> File the residue, then merge the PR.

The residue is the open question raised in `idea-0001` `state/0006.md`, which
recorded it as a bent seam rather than a clean one:

> **The writer seam bent, and it should be named.** Gathering from outside the
> estate is `research`, bound to The Factor. This session's harness forbids
> spawning agents, so the Steward fetched the URL and wrote the concept
> inline. The law's remedy for an unavailable agent is to report the gap, not
> to substitute — reporting it here.

> **Does the writer seam need a stated exception for material the operator
> hands over directly?** T supplying a URL mid-decision is not the same as
> commissioning `research`, but nothing in `system/LAW.md` distinguishes them.

The occasion: T pasted a URL into the conversation to settle a decision in
flight. The Steward read it and used it. Under a literal reading of the law
that was `research` performed inline — the exact failure the binding exists to
prevent. Under a plain reading it was the operator handing over a fact, which
is not a verb at all.

## Tensions

<!-- Unresolved conflicts and COMPETING FRAMINGS — two live readings pulling
     against each other. Distinct from open questions, which await an answer;
     a tension awaits a choice, and may never be resolved at all. -->

- **Two live readings of the same act.** *Reading A:* any material from outside
  the walls entering the estate is `research`, however it arrives, because the
  binding is about who may author from outside sources — and an exception
  invites "the operator mentioned it" as a laundering route for inline work.
  *Reading B:* the verb is a **commission**, not a **channel**; T speaking is
  the operator channel, not the outside world, and treating conversation as
  `research` would make every fact T states a dispatch.
- **A seam that bends under harness constraints is either wrong or
  unenforced.** This session could not spawn agents at all. If the law's rules
  only hold when the harness cooperates, the honest move may be to state what
  the law requires *of the harness*, not only of the Steward — a
  self-containment question (`AGENTS.md`), since the estate is supposed to
  function without any particular environment.
