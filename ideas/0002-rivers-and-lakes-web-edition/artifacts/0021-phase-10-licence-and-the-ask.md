# Phase 10 — Licence research, then the ask

The Surveyor. The Trajectory's tenth leg. It has no dependency in the
build and one in the estate: `research` is a dispatch, and a dispatch
waits on T's word. The Horizon dates only that the ask is made by
2027-03-05.

**Outcome.** The question the Spark put first, answered as far as it
can be from outside, and the ask in the author's hands.

**Waits on.** T's word for the dispatch. Better after Phase 9, so the
ask can say a game has been finished in it.

**When.** On T's word; the ask by 2027-03-05.

## Steps

10.1 **Research, in quarantine.** Findings on: who holds the rights to
Rivers and Lakes; what Tim Hutchings' published terms for Thousand
Year Old Vampire hacks say about a web edition of a hack; how Wym
Lawson has answered others who asked; whether an unlisted URL is
private use under the terms found; where the author can be reached.
Untrusted content stays in the spawned window; the Findings return.

10.2 **The offer's shape.** `decide` in the estate: what the ask offers
(credits and a buy link; a private edition only; a bring-your-own-book
mode; takedown on request), which channel, and what silence means. The
Architect's sixty-day rule is one candidate; T's word settles it.

10.3 **The draft.** The agent writes the ask from the Decision: what
was built, that the text was never public, what is asked, what is
offered, in T's voice and under T's name. Up to the line.

10.4 **Sending.** `**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**` — the channel, the sending and
the content as sent are T's; the ask is an act inside T's relationship
with the author.

10.5 **The answer, recorded.** Whatever arrives enters as `capture`;
terms, refusal or silence past the rule set Phase 11's branch.

## Done when

The ask is sent and its date recorded in the estate; or T records, by
`decide`, that it will not be sent, which voids the Horizon's "for
anyone" half by its own third falsifier.

## Cost

One dispatch, one estate session for the Decision, one sitting of T's.

## Left open inside this Phase

- The sixty-day silence rule, the Architect's, unconfirmed; 10.2 is
  where it is confirmed or struck.
