# Phase 9 — The first finished game

The Surveyor. The Trajectory's ninth leg. Nothing but T's hands can do
the centre of this Phase; the agent's part is what comes back.

**Outcome.** One Xiake, born and ended in the site, and the first two
falsifiers checkable.

**Waits on.** Phases 5, 6, 8.

**When.** December to January.

## Steps

9.1 **Play.** `**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**` — T creates a Xiake, rolls, writes,
and reaches an ending, on the unlisted deploy, at least one sitting on
a phone. The agent prepares nothing; the site is the preparation.

9.2 **Field reports.** Each thing T notices enters the estate as
`capture` on this record (the return path, ADR 0029) or the target's
issue queue, whichever T reaches for; the agent triages and fixes.
Fixes ship as loop ticks under the existing Phases; a fix that changes
a reading goes through the readings register.

9.3 **The book falsifier, dry run.** `**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**` — from the
export, ten ledger entries chosen at random; for each, T points from
the play screens alone to the book's words and the site's reading.
One miss is a labelling defect to fix before 2027-03-05, not yet the
falsifier. The agent draws the ten and records the result.

9.4 **The sheet falsifier, watched.** After the ending, whether the
second Xiake begins in the site or on paper is the first falsifier's
evidence; the agent does nothing but leave the restart nudge where it
is.

## Done when

A save holds a sheet marked ended with its demise written; every
report from 9.1 is fixed or filed with a reason; 9.3 is recorded.

## Cost

T's evenings; a tick per fix.
