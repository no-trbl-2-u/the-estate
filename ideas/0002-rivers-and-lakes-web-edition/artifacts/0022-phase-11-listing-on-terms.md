# Phase 11 — Listing on terms

The Surveyor. The Trajectory's eleventh leg, and the only one with a
branch that ends it unbuilt.

**Outcome.** The site public under the author's terms, credited, with a
link to buy the book; or the site exactly as Phase 8 left it.

**Waits on.** Phase 9 (a finished game); Phase 10 (an answer).

**When.** After the answer; no date.

## Steps

11.1 **The credited build.** Credits to Wym Lawson and Tim Hutchings in
the frame, a link to buy the book, any wording the terms require, and
whatever the terms forbid removed. `noindex` lifted, a sitemap added,
the share renderer's banner kept.

11.2 **Terms as data.** The terms recorded in the target's bearings as
standing decisions and in the estate as a Decision, so the loop never
reinterprets them.

11.3 **Listing.** `**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**` — T is the one answerable for
publishing another author's text under terms; the agent ships 11.1 to
the unlisted URL and T flips it public, announces it where they choose,
or does not.

11.4 **The other branch.** No terms, a refusal, or silence past the rule
from Phase 10: this Phase is closed with the reason, the site stays
unlisted, bring-your-own-book stays on the shelf and is not built. The
build-plan row reads `[skipped]` with the reason, set by T through the
target's oversight.

## Done when

Either the site is listed and the credits are on it, or the Phase is
recorded closed by 11.4.

## Cost

One loop tick and T's word, or none.

## Left open inside this Phase

- Nothing of its own. The identity-against-problem tension is settled
  here by the author, not by the estate.
