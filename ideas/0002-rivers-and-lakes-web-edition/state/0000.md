## Tensions

- **Faithful reproduction against a product that can be published.** The
  site as the paper game, Prompts verbatim — against a site that may not be
  publishable at all because the Prompts and oracles are another author's
  work and no licence was found. Stated in full on `idea.md`.
- **Strict enforcement against the book's own looseness.** A rules engine
  that refuses illegal moves as the whole value of a web edition — against
  the book's "you do not need to answer every question" and "feel free to
  modify the Prompt". Stated in full on `idea.md`.

## Open questions

1. **Licence.** No licence or copyright statement found in the extracted text
   of either PDF. What can ship publicly: Prompts verbatim, Prompt numbers
   only with a "bring your own book" mode, or nothing until permission is
   obtained? T ranks this first: it decides the product. Checkable outside
   the estate; the commissioned `research` covers the reference files only,
   so this remains open after it.
2. **Which mode first**: Quick Game, Journaling Game, or both?
3. **Dice**: in-browser roll, physical dice entered by hand, or both?
4. **Oracles**: shown and rolled on by the site, or only referenced?
5. **The `reference/` repo**: a `research` source only, or is anything to be
   promoted into the estate's own `reference/`?
