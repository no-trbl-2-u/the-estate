# State 0003

## Established

The idea is framed. Underneath "a website version" the operator placed
**access**: the book, playable, for anyone, as faithful as the licence
allows. The mechanism (the sheet keeps itself) and the legacy (the share
page) survive as parts, not as the problem. Scope is the whole game with an
advisory engine; out are accounts, native apps and other games. The
falsifier is personal: abandonment if the operator stops playing it. The
interesting tension is fidelity pulling both ways: the site needs the
author's text to be the book, and must define what the author left to the
player in order to play it.

## Decisions

- **Access over mechanism as the framing.** T chose "the book, playable,
  for anyone" against the Gardener's recommendation. Recorded as
  load-bearing: it is why bring-your-own-book was rejected and why the
  licence question gates publication rather than the build.
- **Generated prose is recorded as an open question, not scope.** T did
  not exclude it when offered alongside three exclusions they did take.
  The Gardener declined to infer either way.
- **The engine's open readings are in scope as labelled readings**, not
  deferred to a later verb; T settled them during `explore`.
