the sheet is a set of tabs, not the interface; the ledger leaves the
screen. The Horizon was re-explored live with four choices taken: one

not shown. The Horizon's date, falsifiers and everything about the

## Decisions

- **The redirect is an `explore` on the Horizon, not a re-frame or a
  re-chart.** The problem, scope and tension in the Framing hold; the
  Trajectory's legs hold; only the picture of the play surface changed.
  Leg 5 is reinterpreted, not rewritten, and `phase` will decompose it
  with the new shape.
- **People are grouped by the book's three types, not by ally and
  enemy.** T's words were "allies, enemies"; the book's Character types
  are reputations, and the Findings say the book refuses black and
  white. Recorded so the choice is visible and reversible.
- **The "book" falsifier moves from the ledger to the dialogue's
  labelling.** T removed the visible ledger; the falsifier was rewritten
  rather than dropped.
- **The interface may teach the game.** T said they do not know the game
  well; a one-line explanation of a trait the first time a beat touches
  it passes the "nothing that answers a Prompt" rule.
- **The first ten layout proposals are superseded**, not kept in the
  record: they were a temporary file at T's request, and the ten that
  replace them are built on the dialogue shape.
