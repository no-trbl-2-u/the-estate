# State 0002

## Established

The Spark was explored with T live, in three rounds of parallel choices,
and a sharper Spark written. The product now has a chosen shape: verbatim
Prompts with permission sought first and everything built privately
meanwhile; both play modes from one engine, switchable any time; an
advisory engine that proposes and flags but never refuses, with every
book-undefined operation named and cited; in-browser dice with manual
entry; oracles rolled in-site; a read-only share page carried in the link
with no server; the operator as first audience. Three rules readings the
book leaves open were settled for the engine: two Technique axes, both
readings of "use a Technique with none Acquired" offered, zero rolls and
clamps counted as landings.

## Decisions

- **Three choices went against the Forager's recommendation** and are
  marked load-bearing in the artifact: verbatim Prompts over
  bring-your-own-book; both modes over journaling-first; a share page over
  export-only; and, in the rules, two Technique axes over one. Recorded
  because a challenge should attack exactly these.
- **The Spark's "nothing added" sentence is left standing** with the two
  chosen exceptions (engine-defined operations, the share page) recorded as
  tensions rather than amended away. Amending it is a `frame` or `decide`
  matter.
- **Variants not chosen are kept on the shelf** in the artifact, with
  bring-your-own-book named as the fallback if permission is refused.

## Tensions

- **Faithful reproduction against a product that can be published.** T chose
  faithful and accepted the block; the fallback is named.
- **"Nothing added" against two chosen additions**: an engine that defines
  what the book left to the player, and a share page. The Spark's sentence
  and the operator's choices disagree; neither has been amended.
- **Strict enforcement against the book's looseness.** Resolved for now by
  the advisory posture; the strict variant is shelved as a setting.
- **Fidelity to the paper game against fidelity to the text file.** Unchanged.
- **Oracle scope.** Unchanged; the Mandarin half is undecided.

## Open questions

1. **Licence**: unanswerable from the sources; needs `research` outside the
   walls, then an outward act that is T's alone. The ask's shape is
   deliberately open.
2. Does the share page belong to "nothing added"?
3. The sheet's layout: the book has none; the site designs it.
4. Is the applied-operation history shown, exportable, or internal?
5. Mandarin oracles: shipped or not?
6. Do an alternate identity's Legends (21A) and a parentless Song (46B)
   count against the slot limits? Not asked; carried from the Findings.
