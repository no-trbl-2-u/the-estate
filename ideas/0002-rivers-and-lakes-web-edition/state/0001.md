## Tensions

- **Faithful reproduction against a product that can be published.**
  Unchanged; the Findings confirm no terms of use exist in the sources.
- **Strict enforcement against the book's own looseness.** Sharpened by the
  Findings: the rules chapter is a small closed engine, but the Prompts
  demand roughly twenty operations the chapter never defines (unstrike,
  retype, convert Character to Resource, uncheck, remove a Scar, edit
  Legends and Songs, Story overrides, whole-sheet effects, deferred locks,
  player-judged selectors). "Nothing added" and a rules engine pull apart
  here: the engine must add definitions the book left to the player.
- **Fidelity to the paper game against fidelity to the text file.** The
  A/B/C letters, the Prompt count of 80, and the entry structure exist in
  the text file and the Spark, not in the printed book.
- **Oracle scope.** The oracles are twice what the Spark described (a full
  Mandarin mirror, weapon and technique naming tables); "show and roll the
  oracles" is larger than assumed and every table is the author's work under
  the same unresolved licence.

## Open questions

1. **Licence.** Still unanswerable from the three files: no copyright,
   licence or permission text; PDF metadata empty; the only attribution is
   the credits page. Needs `research` outside the walls.
2. Do "Righteous Technique" / "Evil Technique" (Prompts 28B, 30B) mean
   Orthodox / Unorthodox? The rulebook never equates them.
3. Which reading of "use a Technique with none Acquired" does the engine
   take: page 15 (use an unacquired one, lose it after) or page 20 (resolve
   the consequences, or lose an unacquired one)?
4. Does an alternate identity's set of Legends (21A) count against the five
   slots, and does 46B's parentless Song count against the four?
5. Skip chains and landing semantics (zero roll, clamp to 1) are unstated;
   the Factor's inferred readings I-1 to I-5 need the operator's
   confirmation.
6. There is no record sheet in the book; the sheet's layout is the site's
   to design.
7. Which mode first, dice, oracles, sharing: answered by T live during
   `explore`, to be folded into that artifact, not here.
