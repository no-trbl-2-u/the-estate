# Phase 3 — Effects and Oracle lines

**Outcome.** Every Technique and Ritual does something the engine can
run, and every Oracle result reads as a line.

**Depends on.** Phase 2.

**Steps.**
1. The effect record shape: id, class (mechanical, combat-narrative,
   exploration, oracle-like, summoning), cost, timing (combat as the
   winner's option; Rituals outside combat, Wudang excepted), the
   engine operation, the authored line, label `invention` with the
   record as its citation.
2. Author the 72 records from Findings §3.2 and §3.3, the eight with
   numbers first (Body and Mind balance, Punishing the sky at half,
   Vital Breath, Chain Fists, Butterfly Palms, Light Body, Lord of the
   Bow, Iron Shirt).
3. Author the 66 Oracle cells and the 11 Unexpected Events as lines.
4. T reads every line as the game's voice and may reserve any; the
   agent drafts all of them. If T reserves, that step carries
   `**[HUMAN ATTENTION]**` for those lines only.
5. Tests: each mechanical record changes state as written; each
   narrative record resolves to an Opening, an escape, or a gate.

**Done looks like.** 149 authored lines in the content package; every
effect record has a test; the count is readable from the build.

**Cost.** Two to three weeks; each record is a design decision.
