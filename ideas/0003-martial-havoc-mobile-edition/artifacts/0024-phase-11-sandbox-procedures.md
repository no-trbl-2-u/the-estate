# Phase 11 — Sandbox procedures

**Outcome.** The world generates, and a Master can walk into it.

**Depends on.** Phases 5, 9, 10.

**Steps.**
1. The generator: N points (a handful, 5–8) on a plane; each linked to
   its nearest one or two; each link's miles as the sum of the two dice
   values and its route band from the same sum (the parent's rule);
   the region screen as an SVG diagram of links, declared not to scale.
   Labelled invention with `I-15` and the parent's rule cited.
2. Region columns: Location at the throw; Landmark, Resources, Risk and
   Event rolled on visit and per move per R51, with the at-throw
   variant as a labelled reading.
3. Monastery generation on the same procedure with its table; road
   features on routes; the five encounter columns; the 36 hooks as a
   menu with a line each.
4. The city procedures from Phase 6 applied to a generated City.
5. Scene transitions: the cave's ending opens the region; continue or
   fresh start per Phase 5.
6. Tests with a seeded scatter: a region throws, a location is entered,
   an encounter resolves, the record carries it.

**Done looks like.** The seeded test passes; a Master leaves the cave
into a region on the web build.

**Cost.** Four to six weeks; the generator is twenty lines, the menus
are the work.
