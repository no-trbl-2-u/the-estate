# Phase 6 — The trail-head village

**Outcome.** Buying, LUCK recovery, meals and rest have a home.

**Depends on.** Phase 2.

**Steps.**
1. The City procedures generalised from the rulebook: Market (buy and
   sell, resale below price), Temple (incense, Spirituality check, one
   success per day per `I-58`), an inn (meals per ration, a night's rest
   at +4 ENDURANCE, a week's rest to full).
2. The village as a content file: three locations, no exploration, the
   trail to the Flat-top mountain.
3. Tests: a Master buys a rope, recovers LUCK, rests to +4.

**Done looks like.** The village loads from data; the three loops run
in tests; every behaviour labelled.

**Cost.** One week.
