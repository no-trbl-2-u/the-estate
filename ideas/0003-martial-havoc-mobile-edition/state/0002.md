# State 0002

## Established

The Spark was explored with T live, in four rounds of parallel choices, and
a sharper Spark written. The product now has a chosen shape: Expo and React
Native for iOS, Android and web over a pure functional engine package with
injected dice; a first release of the whole rulebook's mechanics with The 5
Treasures as its only content; menus and authored short text with an
optional player-written passage; all 72 Technique and Ritual effects
hand-authored under the Factor's five-class scheme; every remaining `I-nn`
reading adopted as a labelled, reversible default; ATT as headcount when a
group and SKILL penalty when alone; one Master roll against each attacker's
own roll; a small data-driven world-state layer for the adventure; the Monk
as a rescue whose killing costs Dishonor; the whole repository CC BY-SA
4.0; in-app dice with manual entry; the operator as first audience with
private builds; the eight printed Masters shipped as printed; a light
three-act frame. Open questions 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 11–14, 16, 17 and 18 from
state 0001 are answered by these choices and dropped below.

## Decisions

- **Two choices went against the Forager's recommendation** and are marked
  load-bearing in the artifact: Heroic Havoc researched before `frame`
  rather than deferred; the eight printed Masters shipped as printed rather
  than corrected. One answer was T's own words rather than an offered
  option (the narration model) and is marked likewise. A `challenge` should
  attack exactly these.
- **`relate` to idea-0002 was chosen** and is performed next as its own
  verb with its own snapshot; the edge is not written here.
- **The Heroic Havoc dispatch is given the word** by T's choice of it. It
  produces Findings that supersede the corresponding `I-nn` defaults where
  the parent rules are explicit.
- **Presets are checked, not gated.** Shipping Yin's overspent sheet means
  creation's rules are reported, not enforced, for presets. The Forager
  notes this as the advisory posture arriving through a side door; whether
  it extends to rolled Masters is `frame`'s.

## Tensions

- **Intentional ambiguity against code that must resolve it.** Resolved
  for now by labelled, reversible readings; the parent-game research may
  replace some readings with rules.
- **Open sandbox against one authored adventure.** T chose the adventure;
  the sandbox is deferred, not refused. Sharpened: the first release has
  no town, so the market, temple and rest loops have no home yet.
- **Advice against enforcement.** Unchanged; the light three-act frame
  overrides no dice.
- **Fidelity to the print against legality**: Yin's sheet ships overspent.
  New, from T's choice.
- **The adventure needs a world-state layer the rulebook has no model
  for.** Accepted as a small explicit layer; the tension is now about its
  size, not its existence.
- **The Spark's counts against the inventory.** Corrected in the explored
  Spark; the first Spark stands.
