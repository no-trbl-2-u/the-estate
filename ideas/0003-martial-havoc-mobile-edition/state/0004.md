## Tensions

- **Intentional ambiguity against code that must resolve it.** Sharpened
  again: most of the 63 ambiguities are the child's inventions, so
  "consult the parent" has a hard ceiling and T remains the rules
  authority.
- **The child's gloss of ATTACK against the parent's.** The record's
  central combat ambiguity is a translation drift, not a design silence;
  T's chosen reading is a house rule if kept.
- **The parent has grown past the child.** v1.6's specification
  sub-tables, Oracle rewrite, Enemy AI and settlement procedures answer
  several ambiguities better than the child's text, which never saw them;
  inheriting from the parent's current version departs from the child.
- **The child's true source may be unrecoverable.** The undefined
  "Special" attack and the reversed `No. of enemies` ordering trace to
  parent v1.5, no longer served anywhere.
- **Art provenance.** The child's English page says no generative AI; its
  Italian page says AI-assisted graphics; the parent's art is DALL-E 3. A
  mobile edition reusing the child's art inherits the contradiction.
- **ShareAlike flows down.** The parent's licence is why the child is
  BY-SA; a mobile edition reproducing rules text is a further derivative
  under the same terms. T already chose whole-repo CC BY-SA, so this
  tension is held rather than live.
- Carried unchanged from state 0002: open sandbox against one adventure;
  advice against enforcement; fidelity to the print against legality; the
  world-state layer's size; the Spark's counts against the inventory.
