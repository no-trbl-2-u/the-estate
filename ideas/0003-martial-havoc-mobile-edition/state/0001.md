## Tensions

- **Intentional ambiguity against code that must resolve it.** Sharpened
  by the Findings: §6 lists 63 decisions, of which roughly 20 are
  load-bearing for combat alone. Stated in full on `idea.md`.
- **Open sandbox against one authored adventure.** Sharpened: region
  generation is a physical dice scatter on paper with "arbitrary" distance;
  any digital substitute is an invention, not an implementation. Stated in
  full on `idea.md`.
- **Advice against enforcement.** Sharpened: the book's advice (do not end
  fights with Techniques; "the greatest power is to refrain") is guidance
  the engine cannot enforce without contradicting R25b, which makes
  Technique use a legal winner's option. Stated in full on `idea.md`.
- **The adventure needs a world-state layer the rulebook has no model
  for.** Day/night via the gourd, Ogres hunting "once a day", the Junior
  King's nap, spoiler-gated Hints, a locked door with a key: "the
  rulebook's engine plus The 5 Treasures as content" is still more than the
  rulebook's engine.
- **ATT in the adventure against ATT in the roster.** The Woodgatherer's
  and Skillful Beast's ATT 5 make the ATTACK rule matter far more than the
  rulebook's own roster does; the two readings of I-05 produce very
  different difficulty.
- **The Spark's counts against the inventory.** Small miscounts (45 versus
  50 stat blocks; four "d66" tables that are banded), but a rules engine
  built from the Spark rather than the Findings would mis-shape four tables.
