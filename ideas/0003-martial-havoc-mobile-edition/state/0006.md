# State 0006

## Established

The idea is framed. Underneath "a mobile game version" T placed, in their
own words, **"A rules engine for Martial Havoc for a way to create
multiple Wuxia style dnd gameplay"**, and chose the reading that **the
sandbox is the real game and adventures are scenes in it**. The engine is
the product; The 5 Treasures is the first island; the rulebook's region
generation, monasteries, hooks and encounter matrices are the end state.
Out of scope, certain: accounts and sync, multiplayer, generated prose,
money. The falsifier is T's own: "if I stop playing it myself." The
rulebook's market, temple, meals and night's rest get a home in a
trail-head village at the foot of Flat-top mountain. The interesting
tension: an engine that must resolve everything, built for a book that
delegates by design, sharpened by the sandbox being the part the book
describes least.

## Decisions

- **Engine over book as the framing.** T wrote their own answer rather
  than take any of the four offered, then chose the sandbox reading
  against the Gardener's recommendation (adventures as data with a
  persistent Master). Both are load-bearing; a `challenge` should attack
  the sandbox-as-real-game claim first, because the first release ships
  none of it.
- **First-release scope is unchanged** by the framing: engine plus The 5
  Treasures plus the village. The sandbox is named as owed, not scheduled.
- **The trail-head village is added to the first release** by T's word,
  answering state 0002's first open question.
