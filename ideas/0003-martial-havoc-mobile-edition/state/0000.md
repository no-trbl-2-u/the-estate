# State 0000

## Established

The record exists: `idea-0003`, root scope, no project. Its Spark is the
operator's prompt verbatim — a mobile game version of Martial Havoc
(Gianluca Monaco; a hack of Heroic Havoc, Ruolatori Solitari) where the
phone is the Master's sheet, the dice, the oracle and the Game Master; the
full mechanics implemented; The 5 Treasures as the first adventure shipped.
The Spark names two PDFs as the rules, records their licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)
and credits, carries T's own reading of the rulebook and of the adventure,
four standing constraints (functional style, explicit per-function
documentation, offline with no server, attribution shipped in the game),
eight open questions and three tensions.

The two source files are **inside the estate**, at
`ideas/0003-martial-havoc-mobile-edition/sources/`: `MH_Full_Itchio.pdf`
(sha256 `d4052004…`) and `The-5-treasures.pdf` (sha256 `8303fb15…`),
byte-identical to the repository copies, each with a plain-text extraction
beside it (`*.extracted.txt`, made with pdf-parse 1.1.1; derived, not
source — where the text and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins).

## Decisions

- **Root `ideas/`, not a project, and not `/onboard`.** The PDFs are another
  author's game documents, source material for `research`; `onboard` would
  have typed them as T's own mature work. T stated the scope in the prompt.
- **Appetite 2.** Hand-set. The prompt arrived with a route chosen, the word
  given for two boundaries, and the rulebook carried in by hand; that is
  heat.
- **The prompt is the Origin and the Spark both.** T adopted a drafted
  prompt as their own words by passing it to `/start`; the Origin records
  how it arrived, the Spark records it unaltered.
- **The sources live in the record, under `sources/`, not in `reference/`.**
  T's word was "bring the rulebook into idea estate". `reference/` is the
  OKF bundle — it describes the estate and holds promoted concepts, not
  another author's PDFs — and idea-0002 kept its reference repo outside for
  want of a home. A record-local `sources/` keeps the record self-contained
  (a stranger can run `research` from the record alone) and touches no
  law: the validator reads `artifacts/*.md` and `state/` only. It is a
  **new file convention**, adopted on T's word for this record; `ideas/
  README.md` does not list it, and making it general is an ADR. Both
  PDFs came in, not the rulebook alone: the Spark names both as the rules,
  and the adventure is the first release's content. The Steward's
  inference, confidence 60; T may strike the adventure or move the
  directory.

## Tensions

- **Intentional ambiguity against code that must resolve it.** The book's
  gaps as design, against a phone that cannot shrug. Stated in full on
  `idea.md`.
- **Open sandbox against one authored adventure.** Regions, oracles and
  thirty-six hooks as the game, against fixed areas and tables as the only
  finishable content. Stated in full on `idea.md`.
- **Advice against enforcement.** "Do not use the Technique to end a fight"
  and "refrain" as the style the game teaches, against advice to a solo
  player that stops being advice once the engine imposes it. Stated in full
  on `idea.md`.

## Open questions

1. **Platform and stack.** Targets and technology undecided; T asked that
   nothing be assumed from other projects in the estate.
2. **Scope of "full mechanics" for the first release**: the whole rulebook
   including the sandbox, or the engine with The 5 Treasures as the only
   content.
3. **How the game handles what the book leaves to imagination**: automated
   narration, player-typed journal, menu choices, or a mix.
4. **ShareAlike reach.** Does CC BY-SA 4.0 reach the game's source code, and
   is that acceptable.
5. **Images.** The cave map and the eight pre-generated Masters are images
   only: extract, redraw, or omit.
6. **Promotion to `reference/`.** Answered in part this session: the PDFs
   are in the record's `sources/`; whether anything is promoted to
   `reference/` as a concept stays open, per the `research` skill.
7. **Relation to idea-0002** (Rivers and Lakes, web edition): a `relate`
   edge, a shared engine, or nothing.
8. **The Devil servant's loot on a 6** is an icon with no text. Needs a
   reading.
