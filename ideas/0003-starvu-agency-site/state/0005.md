# State 0005

## Established

Two `research` dispatches, both to The Factor: a **functional inventory** of all
three references, gathered at T's request as raw material for a roadmap the
Surveyor will build later; and a **close study of one page** T pointed at
mid-session.

Screenshots failed a third and fourth time despite the Steward opening a Browser
pane. Everything below is **measured, not seen** — computed styles, DOM, and
source bundles. The Factor marked every appearance claim as inference, and its
own caution stands: *"'reads as confident' is what measurement is worst at."*

### The functional picture

- **Foxy's homepage makes zero `fetch()` calls.** A static Netlify site. The
  "LIVE DASHBOARD" is static markup. The social-proof toasts are a hardcoded
  array, capped at three per session. The ROI calculator is a client-side lookup
  table whose own source comment describes its growth tiers as *"realistic and
  believable."*
- **Foxy's "Ask us anything…" chat input has no JavaScript behind it anywhere on
  the site.** Not disabled, no handler, belongs to no form. **A live agency
  shipped a chat affordance with no chat.** None of the three runs a real chat
  vendor — all three route conversational intent to WhatsApp.
- **Two of three have an earnings calculator**, not one: AT has `#estimator`
  (two sliders plus a Low/Medium/High toggle). A calculator is category
  furniture, not a differentiator. This corrects the Steward's handoff packet.
- **Only Foxy exposes a calendar at all**, inline and ungated — bookable before
  any form, with the form's success modal handing off *to* it. Eros and AT
  collect contact details and schedule by hand. **T's North Star's terminal
  action is the category's outlier.**
- **Zero of three have a working creator portal.** Eros's is the only one that
  exists: Webflow Memberships switched on, live, unstyled, titled *"Copy of
  Template,"* linked from nothing. Its `/components` page still carries German
  boilerplate about a Dortmund landscaping firm.
- **"Content protection" appears in the reference set exactly once** — as a
  dropdown option on AT's application form. Nobody sells it or explains it.
- **AT's real statistics: 4+ years, 24/7 support, 12+ creators managed.** By far
  the smallest claims in the set, and stated on the hero.

### The page T pointed at

**`/book-a-call` is a hard 404.** The nav *label* is "BOOK A CALL"; the
destination is `/contact`. The Factor researched `/contact`. The 404 page is
itself designed — navy, Playfair 36px, one CTA — but it is a stub.

`/contact`: **5 sections, 5.94 desktop screens, 5 images** against the
homepage's 17 sections, 22.3 screens, 68 images. **The same company at one
quarter the length.** No testimonials, no logo wall, no pricing, no calculator,
no dashboard, no toasts, no host bio. The FAQ is not an accordion — all four
answers render open.

## Decisions

- **The Steward opened the Browser pane but did not look through it.** Opening
  the pane is a harness action; reading the page is The Factor's office
  (`system/LAW.md`). The gate was opened; the Steward did not walk through. It
  did not help — the pane still would not composite for a subagent.
- **Both dispatches ran on a partial instrument rather than aborting.**
  Measurement without pixels has now produced five artifacts of real substance.

## Gold nuggets

- **"They spent the whole motion budget on the payoff, not the hero."** The
  custom easing curve exists twice in 38,859 characters of CSS, and both
  instances are the post-submit success modal — a staggered self-drawing SVG
  check on a warm `#F8F6F0` card reading **"You're in the room."**
- **Italic Playfair Display at weight 500, for every display element, 17
  selectors, no exceptions.** Not one roman serif heading, not one bold one.
  *Bold serif reads newspaper; light italic serif reads invitation.* The Factor
  ranks this first among the choices doing the work, and not closely.
- **Subtraction as the system.** Twenty CSS custom properties are the entire
  palette-and-scale vocabulary. Two shadows, two durations, two hover lift
  depths, four accent-colour instances on the whole page — and **zero scroll
  reveals, no entrance animation of any kind.**
- **The calendar is stock Calendly with zero custom UI.** Themed by two query
  params (`primary_color=0d1129`, `hide_gdpr_banner=1`). The bespoke feel is
  `border-radius: 24px` + `overflow: hidden` + one shadow on the wrapper. **Two
  properties.** The event slug is still `welcome-to-your-new-agency-clone` —
  Calendly's duplicate suffix, never renamed, public in the source.
- **"Roughly 80% of the effect is configuration, not craft."** Cheap to
  reproduce: the token block, the italic-500 serif voice, two shadows, the 96px
  rhythm, the pill buttons, the entire calendar treatment. Costs real time: the
  modal choreography, and **the photography — the one line item no CSS
  rescues.**
- **A real defect, and a pointed one.** On mobile the calendar card is 303px;
  Calendly's hardcoded `min-width: 320px` renders at 320px; `overflow: hidden`
  amputates **17px off the right edge** of the booking UI. No page-level
  scrollbar, because the card swallows it. **The choice that makes the page look
  expensive is the same choice that breaks its only job on a phone.**

## Tensions

- **✨expensive✨ versus *confident* — first evidence that they may be one
  target, not two.** The booking page keeps every expensive surface and discards
  all the explaining, and the expensive feeling got *stronger*, because the
  restraint and the palette are the same gesture. The Factor's reading: the
  homepage is simply where Foxy failed to hold it. Flagged with its own caution
  — this is measurement reasoning about feel. **The tension is not resolved, but
  it now has a candidate resolution: brevity is not the enemy of confidence;
  over-explaining is.**
- **Mark versus feeling** — untouched by these dispatches.

## Open questions

- **Which page did T actually have open?** `/book-a-call` 404s. If "fancy
  shmancy" was said at the 404 stub rather than at `/contact`, the study aimed
  at the wrong target. **This is a question for T and it is cheap to answer.**
- **What is inside the Calendly flow?** The Factor saw only the outside — did
  not open, select, or submit. Every step after "Select a Date & Time," the
  required fields, timezone handling, availability display, and confirmation are
  explicitly untested.
- **Does the AI stock read as generated on screen?** Still needs pixels.
- **Was the friend's negative brief aimed at the category or at his own flyer?**
  Carried. Still the cheapest unanswered question, and still a conversation
  rather than a dispatch.
- **Which feeling leads?** Now better informed, still undecided.
- **Gate the calendar or leave it bare?** Sharpened considerably: the category
  gates it, Foxy does not, and T's North Star wants the booking.
- **Would the friend state the same North Star?** Carried, unasked.
- **Where do the asset binaries live?** Carried.
- **`appetite: 1` is still a placeholder.**
