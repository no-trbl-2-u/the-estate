# State 0004

## Established

T widened the grant to `tools: *`. **The browser opened on the first call.**
Four dispatches in, the reference sites are finally in the record as measured
things.

The instrument is not the one the packet expected, and the difference governs
everything: **The Factor measured these pages; it did not see them.** Pages
loaded and executed JavaScript; computed styles, DOM, viewport emulation, and
ref-based interaction all worked. **Every screenshot failed** — four attempts,
`"the Browser pane is not displayed, so the page is not compositing frames."`
The Factor ran the dispatch anyway, correctly: computed styles are a genuine
advance over text, and it held the seam visibly rather than quietly upgrading
measurement into observation.

**AT Agency rendered for the first time in four passes.**

### The three sites, as measured

- **Foxy Studios** — `#FFFFFF` ground, `#0D1129` navy, **Playfair Display**
  serif with Inter, pill buttons. **The most expensive-looking surface in the
  set by a clear margin.** 17 sections; **22.3 phone screens** tall.
- **Eros Agency** — `#050505` with `#EC1194` hot magenta, magenta-to-amber
  gradients, a tiled SVG film-grain overlay, all-caps Lexend. **Squarely the
  dark/neon register.** 20.5 phone screens.
- **AT Agency** — `#000000` with a single electric blue `#4DB8FF`, radial
  glows, Montserrat 800 at −5% tracking. **Dark tech-SaaS, not sleazy.**
  **13.4 phone screens** — the shortest by a wide margin.

## Decisions

- **The dispatch ran on a partial instrument rather than aborting.** The stop
  instruction covered "cannot render at all," not "can render but not
  photograph." The Factor judged the advance real and flagged the limit in
  every affected claim. That was the right call.
- **The grant stays at `tools: *`.** It delivered where a named allowlist
  failed twice.

## Gold nuggets

- **"Foxy is an expensive-looking site doing an inexpensive amount of
  explaining."** The Factor's corrected claim — and it did all three things to
  its own standing inference at once. **Overturned on surface:** it predicted
  the wrong palette outright; serif-on-navy-on-white is genuinely expensive.
  **Confirmed on structure:** 17 sections, an ROI calculator, a mock dashboard,
  22.3 screens of scroll. Expensive things under-explain, and Foxy explains
  enormously.
- **The record's two feeling-words do not converge, and now there is evidence.**
  ✨expensive✨ points at Foxy's typography. *Confident* points at nothing
  cleanly — **the site with the most trustworthy type has the least trustworthy
  proof volume.** Two targets, not one.
- **Two of the three references appear to manufacture their proof.** Foxy's
  homepage photography is largely `freepik__`-prefixed AI stock; AT's "As Seen
  In" logos are named `imageye___-_imgi_2_xbiz-logo-white_...` — the filename
  convention of a bulk image-scraper extension. Both inferred from naming and
  marked as such. The category's proof furniture is substantially staged.
- **13.4 phone screens against 22.3 and 20.5.** AT is the only reference short
  enough to survive having no proof — which is exactly Starvu's position.

## Tensions

- **Expensive versus confident.** Newly promoted from open question to live

## The gap, narrowed to one thing

Everything the record wanted is now answered **except appearance-as-experienced**.
Screenshots failed because the Browser pane was not displayed in the subagent's
session, so nothing composited. Two knock-ons: `IntersectionObserver` never
fired, so **Foxy's photography never loaded** (57 of 68 images) and **AT's
count-up statistics stayed at `0+` / `$0M+`**. The `freepik__` question — does
the AI stock *read* as generated on screen — remains open.

A remedy exists and is untested: **the Steward can open the Browser pane in the
main session first**, so a pane exists and composites, then re-dispatch. Opening
the pane is a harness action, not gathering — the Steward would open the gate,
not walk through it. Worth one attempt if T wants pixels.
