# State 0010

## Established

The record has the roadmap T asked for when he opened it. The Surveyor charted
the route from here to the six-month page, classified `trajectory: actionable`.

### The shape of the route

**It is not a page built forward.** It is a short errand that gates months, then
a launch, then **a long quiet middle in which the correct amount of visible page
progress is zero** while four fuses burn, then two harvests where the page grows
in bursts as facts arrive.

**The middle is the part a naive roadmap gets wrong.** Four of the six "real
soon" features need a tool built *and then left running*, so filling that month
with page work delivers them a month late.

### The ordering rule the Surveyor added

The Forager ranked tools by **facts per hour**. That is the right ranking for
value, **not for order.** Every supply-line tool has a **build cost** (T's time,
buyable back) and a **fuse length** (elapsed calendar, not buyable back).

> **Fuse dominates: light the long fuses first, even the cheap ones.**

One item moves materially: **the response-time meter goes from sixth to second.**
Same afternoon, one month earlier — it had been ranked by cost rather than by
how long it must sit still afterwards.

### The legs

| Leg | Gated by |
|---|---|
| **0. The errand** (minutes) | nothing |
| **1. Ship v1** (in flight) | Leg 0's two publication answers, for features 1–2 only |
| **2. Light the fuses** — notes capture, response-time meter, claims ledger (an afternoon each) | the meter needs an exportable support channel — his operational call, not a build |
| **3. The quiet month** — takedown assist, verification tracker (one weekend) | Checkpoint A for the tracker; Leg 0's service answer for takedown |
| **4. First harvest** — the page grows in bursts | each row's own fuse; **photographs have none and can land any week** |
| **5. The portal** — a spur, not trunk | second signed creator + one repeating deliverable; tracker and numbers pack existing; the Chancellor's tension resolved **before** the weekend is spent |
| **6. Months 4–6** — down, or out | Checkpoint C |

### How the two tracks interleave

They do not alternate. **The tooling runs *ahead* of the page by one ripening
period.** Leg 2 is entirely invisible to a visitor and exists solely so Leg 4 has
anything to publish. Leg 3 is one weekend of build and a month of nothing.

The tracks touch at **exactly one point**, in Leg 1: fifteen minutes of
instrumentation — the per-channel link tags and the "How did you hear about
Starvu?" field — page work that serves only the checkpoint track.

> **The strongest instruction on the route: do not ship v1 without that
> instrumentation.** It is fifteen minutes, it is invisible, it is the first
> thing cut under launch pressure, and cutting it makes the warm/cold branch
> permanently unsettleable — the exact `unfalsifiable-by-construction` failure
> `0009` §4 diagnosed.

## Checkpoints, placed where they fall

- **A — week two, the tooling bet.** Deletes the class of tools requiring a
  habit; keeps the passive observers.
- **B — one month of takedown work.** **Can *shrink* the page** — removes content
  protection from the day-one services block and from the portal.
- **C — ~20 bookings, the traffic read.** Decides Leg 6. Fires on booking volume,
  not calendar, so may precede or follow Leg 4.

  destination rather than the path.** If it fires: back to `envision`, and the
  supply line survives intact.

  is a build step that has been failing loudly all along; if not, it is ten

  only.** One consequence nobody had named: if the flyer's register wins, the
  `$15K–$30K` band rides the hero, **the disclaimer becomes mandatory, and that

- **Decision 2 (gate or bare)** bites at Leg 1 **and at Checkpoint C**, with a
  cost never weighed: **a gate lengthens the fuse on the traffic checkpoint** and
  biases its answers toward people willing to clear gates. Offered as input, not
  taken.
- **The portal tension** bites at **Leg 5 and nowhere else.** Uphold `0010`'s
  refusal whole and the spur is deleted with nothing else touched. Do not force

  breach-minimising rules are not additions to a built portal.**

  only in Leg 6.

## The cheapest items that gate the most

**Leg 0 in its entirety** — four questions in one text, the verification vendor
decision, and writing down **N** (how many creators he actually works with).

assistant, the split table, the portal's first three rows, and **the only

is the sole item where delay compounds, and the cost is somebody else's safety.
