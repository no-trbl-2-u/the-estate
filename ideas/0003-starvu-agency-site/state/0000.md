# State 0000

## Established

The record exists, the origin is recorded verbatim, the operator-supplied
material is catalogued, and **`capture` ran** — The Gardener wrote the record's
first artifact (`0001-starvu-agency-site.md`, `type: Spark`). This is the first
artifact anywhere in the estate; `idea-0001` and `idea-0002` still hold none.

What the record now holds: a website for T's friend's new talent-management
business (Starvu LLC, Pennsylvania — promotion for models and OnlyFans
creators); a negative visual brief; a supplied mark and flyer; and three
reference URLs that nobody has looked at.

## Decisions

- **Started at `capture`, not at `frame` or `research`.** T released the first
  move ("I'll let you decide where to start"). Framing before the raw material
  is durable would have framed a paraphrase; fetching the references before the
  thought is recorded would have let three other agencies' sites set the terms
  of an idea that has not yet stated its own. Origin first, then the Spark.
- **The assets are catalogued in prose, not as files.** The images arrived as
  conversation attachments and are not on disk. `assets/MANIFEST.md` names the
  expected filenames and records what each image carries — palette, type,
  voice, compliance furniture — so the record survives if the binaries never
  land. The Gardener was told to treat that prose as the supplied material and
  invent nothing beyond it.
- **The three URLs were held, not fetched.** Reaching outside the walls is
  `research`, bound to The Factor. The Steward does not fetch.

## Gold nuggets

- **"✨expensive✨"** — not *luxury*, not *premium*. The sparkles are part of
  the word; they carry the aspiration and a wink at the aspiration at once.
  And it is relayed: "as my partner says." A third voice, one step out.
- **"We don't want to go super dark, neon lights and sleazy"** — a negative
  brief. It names the industry default and steers off it without naming the
  replacement.
- **"You make the content. We run the business around it."** — already written,
  on the flyer. A division of labor and a promise in nine words. Not something
  to invent.
- The flyer already carries compliance furniture. Someone thought about this
  before the website existed.
- This record does double duty: a real site for a real friend, and the first
  live exercise of the estate. Both carry weight.

## Tensions

Carried in `idea.md` and restated in the Spark, unresolved by design: **the
supplied mark and the stated feeling pull apart.** Black-and-gold star-burst
sports-broadcast energy against "not dark, ✨expensive✨". Either the site
inherits the mark and softens around it, or it sets a new register and the mark
becomes the odd element. The Gardener adds one observation: the friend may not
have noticed the pull yet.

## Open questions

- **What do the three reference sites actually look like?** Unfetched. "The
  type of site they are trying to build" is currently only in T's and the
  friend's heads, not in the record. This is the largest gap.
- **Where do the asset binaries live?** The manifest names expected filenames;
  the files are pending placement by T.
- **Does the flyer's compliance furniture constrain the site?** The Gardener
  recorded that it exists and left the question — it is a `frame` question.
- **Whose site is this?** Recruitment (talent applying) and clientele are
  different audiences with different pages. The flyer is pure recruitment. Not
  yet asked.
- **`appetite: 1` is a placeholder.** Hand-set heat is T's alone.

## Current state declaration

A live record with a faithful Spark and one clean fracture running through it.
Everything in the record is either T's words, his friend's words, or a
description of material they handed over — no framing, no design, no outside
influence has entered yet. The next move is the one that closes the most gap:
the visual target the whole brief points at has not been observed. Until The
Factor fetches the three references, any `frame` would be framing around a
hole.
