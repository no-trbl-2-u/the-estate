# State 0001

## Established

**The record has a stated purpose.** T declared a North Star and scoped it to
the entire idea, not to a page. The Gardener captured it as a second Spark
(`0002-north-star.md`); the first Spark is untouched and is not superseded.

The site's job, in T's own words: an adult film creator arrives, **feels
confident** that Starvu can advise and support them in growing their platform
accounts, **learns the services** — "chatting, advising, content protection,
etc." — and **books a time on the calendar for a 1:1 consultation**. That last
one is flagged "ultimately."

Everything the record held before this was *material*. This is the first
statement of intent, and the first thing a visitor is supposed to **do**.

## Decisions

- **Held as a second Spark, not written into the origin.** The origin is
  never edited. New material arriving from the operator is `capture`, bound to
  The Gardener — so I dispatched rather than transcribing it into state myself.
  The North Star is now a first-class artifact with its own lineage.

## Gold nuggets

- **"feel confident"** — and this is the record's *second* feeling-word. The
  friend supplied ✨expensive✨; T supplies *confident*. The Gardener flags
  that these are not obviously the same target. Expensive is a register;
  confident is a judgment the visitor makes about competence. A site can hit
  one and miss the other.
- **"adult film creators"** — a different phrase for the same population than
  the record's earlier "models, onlyfans models, etc." More specific about the
  work, less tied to a single platform. Both phrasings now sit in the record,
  unreconciled.
- **"chatting, advising, content protection, etc."** — the first named
  services anywhere on the record. The "etc." is explicit; the list is open by
  construction.
- The four-beat path the North Star draws: **arrive → feel confident → learn
  the services → book the 1:1.**

## Tensions

- **Mark versus feeling — carried, and now under load.** The North Star does
  not release the fracture from state/0000; it presses on it. "Feel confident"
  is a stated requirement with a way to fail, and a four-beat path to a booking
  has to carry a visitor the whole distance without losing them. Still
  unchosen.
- **Recruit or sell? — sharpened, not settled.** T named the audience: the
  visitor is an adult film creator, the same population the flyer recruits.
  But the flyer's funnel is *apply and sign*, and the North Star's funnel is
  *book a consultation*. A consultation is not a signing. Two funnels, one
  audience, one site — and no choice made about whether the site runs both.

## Open questions

- **What do the three reference sites actually look like?** Still unfetched.
  Unchanged from state/0000, and still the largest gap.
- **Would the friend state the same North Star?** The Gardener's catch, and a
  good one: this is the first material on the record that comes *from* T
  rather than *through* him — "What **I** want them to do." The negative brief
  and ✨expensive✨ are the friend's and the partner's. Whose North Star this
  is has not been asked.
- **Do ✨expensive✨ and "confident" point at the same site?** Newly opened.
- **Does the flyer's compliance furniture constrain the site?** Carried.
- **Where do the asset binaries live?** Carried; still pending placement.
- **`appetite: 1` is still a placeholder.** Hand-set heat is T's alone.

## Current state declaration

The record now has a floor and a ceiling: faithful raw material underneath, a
stated purpose over the top, and two live fractures running between them —
mark-versus-feeling, and recruit-versus-sell. Nothing has been framed, designed,
or fetched. What the North Star changed is that the gaps are now *measurable*:
every future move on this record can be asked whether it gets a creator closer
to feeling confident and booking the 1:1. The three reference sites remain
unobserved, and that is still the cheapest large gap to close.
