# State 0006

## Established

The research phase closed and The Gardener framed the record. `0008-framing.md`
is the first artifact on this record that is neither raw material nor gathered
fact — it is the ground the Architect and the Surveyor will stand on.

T set the frame's job in his own words: a **long-term roadmap** for the friend's
web presence, whose **first step is a landing page** where a creator can contact
him and book a time. T is standing up the repo now and wants something soon.

### What the framing found

- **The two horizons separate cleanly, and the seam has a name.** The near
  horizon is not a small version of the far one — **it is the only step that
  must stand alone.** A landing page with a booking on it is a complete business
  instrument by itself; everything later is an addition to a working machine.
  The Gardener turned that into a constraint: **nothing in the near horizon may
  be a stub that only makes sense once something later exists.** The record
  supplies its own warning — Foxy's chat input with no chat, Eros's unlinked
  "Copy of Template" login stack. The same mistake twice, and Starvu can afford
  it least.
- **The problem underneath is a proof floor, not an aesthetic.** Starvu is
  *below* the floor of the category's most modest competitor: AT states **4+
  years, 12+ creators** on its hero and Starvu cannot claim even that. Three
  things on the record stop being separate concerns once that is named — the
  category's proof furniture is **a trap rather than a template** (available to
  copy, unavailable to *mean*, to an audience equipped to check); **"feel
  confident" is the substitute for a track record**, not a preference, so it has
  an operational way to fail; and **brevity becomes an advantage rather than a
  deficit**, because a business with nothing to prove has less to say by nature.
- **A hole nobody had marked: nothing anywhere says how a creator arrives.** The
  North Star opens with "adult film creators visit the site" and treats arrival
  as given. Every reference treats it as the hard part — Foxy's ~115 posts and
  its AI-crawler acquisition strategy, AT's paid traffic and referral
  attribution. **Starvu's only distribution mechanism in evidence is a QR
  placeholder on a flyer someone must already be holding.** Named as a hole, not
  a proposal: a page for warm traffic and a page for cold traffic are different
  pages, and nobody has said which this is.

## Decisions

- **`frame` ran before `decide`.** The three live choices were handed to the
  Gardener as choices to *frame*, not to take. Deciding without framed ground
  would have made them preference questions; framed, decision 3 turns out to
  have a dependency the record had not noticed (below).
- **The commit was made; the push was blocked.** `271337e` holds the record
  through initial research plus The Factor's widened grant. The auto-mode
  classifier denied `git push`, so no PR exists yet. Recorded rather than worked
  around; the commands are with T.

## Gold nuggets

- **"The near horizon is not a small version of the far one — it is the only
  step that must stand alone."**
- **"Available to copy, unavailable to *mean*, to an audience equipped to
  check."** The proof-furniture trap, stated exactly.
- **"A better page makes failure faster, not smaller."** The site's job ends at
  the booking; whether the friend can convert a booked call is an assumption the
  site cannot help with.
- **"A site carrying less compliance language than the flyer feeding it got less
  careful as it got more public."** The Gardener's argument for putting the
  compliance surface *inside* the near horizon rather than on the line.
- **The distribution hole:** a QR placeholder on a flyer someone must already be
  holding.

## Two framing calls made explicitly, so they can be attacked

1. **The flyer's funnel and the North Star's are consecutive beats of
   `apply → qualify → free call → sign`, not competitors.** The record should
   stop treating them as a fork. What is genuinely unsettled is smaller: whether
   the site **fills a calendar and lets the conversation close**, or
   **pre-qualifies until the conversation is a formality**.
2. **The compliance surface is *in* the near horizon**, on a factual argument
   rather than a preference: the flyer already carries 18+/ID verification,
   per-partner verification, earnings-not-typical, Fenix non-affiliation, and
   the deferred Talent Management Agreement; zero of three references carry any
   of it.

Two items were deliberately left **on the line** rather than ruled, because they
are design calls and design is The Architect's: the **earnings calculator**
(category furniture, two of three, an afternoon's work) and a **services page**
as a route.

## Tensions

The three live choices are set out in the Framing with what hangs on each, and
**none is taken** — `decide` is The Chancellor's.

1. **Which feeling leads — ✨expensive✨ or *confident*?** Reframed by the
   proof-floor finding: confidence is no longer a preference but the substitute
   for a track record.
2. **Gate the calendar, or leave it bare?** Now the smaller question named
   above.
3. **Inherit the mark, or set a new register?** **And the framing found a
   dependency nobody had noticed:** the "~80% is configuration, not craft"
   finding describes *Foxy's* system, so it transfers **only if the Foxy half of
   the friend's input wins.** Decision 3 is upstream of the cheap-to-reproduce
   claim, not independent of it.
