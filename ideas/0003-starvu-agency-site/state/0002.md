# State 0002

## Established

The Factor went outside the walls and read the three reference sites T
supplied. Findings are in `0003-reference-sites-findings.md`, with its limits
stated as loudly as its results — which is the right proportion here, because
**the largest limit is that the visual question did not get answered.**

What the estate now knows:

- **Foxy Studios** — a mature company's long-scroll conversion page. ~18
  sections, named and photographed leadership, eight named press outlets,
  published pricing (flat 45% agency fee), an earnings calculator, a competitor
  comparison table. Hero: *"An OnlyFans agency built by women, for the top
  0.1%"* / *"4 spots remaining."* Calendly sits in the persistent nav — one
  click to convert, no scrolling required.
- **Eros Agency** — half the size, anonymous team, no address, no terms link.
  Leads with revenue and pivots hard to discretion.
- **AT Agency** — not reached. Fully client-rendered; the Factor's retrieval
  returned `ECONNRESET` on three attempts. Six real routes and 33 SEO blog
  posts recovered from `sitemap.xml`; no services, about, team, or contact
  page found.

## Gold nuggets

- **Eros Agency's hero is "You Create, We Handle Everything Else."** Starvu's
  flyer says *"You make the content. We run the business around it."* The
  record's single best existing sentence is **a category convention, not a
  differentiator.** The Gardener called that line "already written, not
  something to invent" — it turns out it was already written by someone else
  too.
- **"Copying Foxy's proof furniture without its proof reads as false — the
  fastest way to lose 'feel confident.'"** The Factor's line, and the sharpest
  thing on this record. Foxy earns its comparison tables and press logos with
  five years and real numbers. Starvu has neither yet.
- **The compliance instinct is ahead of the references, not behind them.** No
  age gate, no non-affiliation statement, and no earnings-not-typical qualifier
  on *any* of the three. Starvu's flyer already carries all three.
- **Content protection appears on none of them, under any name.** One of T's
  three named services has no competitor counterpart in this sample.
- **"Expensive things under-explain."** The Factor's reasoning for doubting
  that Foxy reads expensive — eighteen sections and an ROI calculator are the
  furniture of persuasion.

## Decisions the findings make available (not yet taken)

- **The recruit-versus-sell tension is dissolved, not resolved.** All three
  sites route to `/apply`, and what the application buys is a *call*: "Apply
  now for your free strategy session," "Get Your Free Strategy Call." The
  category runs **apply → qualify → free call → sign** as one funnel. The

  What remains is a narrower and answerable question: **gate the calendar

  Foxy, the most established, lets a stranger book directly.

## The gap, reported not filled

**`research` ran, and its bound agent could not see.** The Factor's retrieval
converts HTML to text — no JavaScript, no rendered pixels, no computed styles.
Palette, typography, motion, and density were unrecoverable on all three sites,
and AT Agency was unreachable entirely. The record's open fracture is visual;
this artifact does not close it.

This is a capability gap in a binding, which `system/LAW.md` says to report
rather than route around. The Steward has browser tools; reaching outside the
walls is The Factor's, and the Steward does not fetch. Three honest remedies,
T's to choose: grant The Factor a browser and re-dispatch `research`; T looks
at the three sites himself and hands over what he sees (operator-supplied
material, the seam `idea-0002` exists to examine); or the record proceeds
without the visual answer and `frame` works around the hole.

No age gate, consent wall, or verification step was encountered on any site,
and nothing was bypassed.
