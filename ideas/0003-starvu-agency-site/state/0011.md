# State 0011

## Established

The Trajectory is decomposed. **Seven Phases, one artifact each** — `system/TYPES.md`
is explicit for a decomposer (*one `type: Phase` artifact per part, each with
`inputs:` pointing at the decomposed parent*), so a single combined document
would have been the wrong object regardless of convenience. Lineage reads as a
clean seven-way fan-out from `0012`.

The Surveyor stopped at seven deliberately: each Phase is startable as written,
and **decomposing further would have produced dust — task lists inside a plan
that deliberately refuses to be a schedule.**

| # | Phase | Trigger | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The errand | nothing — Monday morning, from a phone | an afternoon, mostly waiting |
| 2 | Ship v1, instrumented | the v1 design returning from flight — already happening | **weeks**; the only multi-week Phase |
| 3 | Light the fuses | the friend's next call (item 1); page live (items 2–3) | three afternoons over two weeks |
| 4 | The quiet month | Checkpoint A resolving | two weekends inside a month of nothing |
| 5 | First harvest | no single trigger — whichever fuse burns down first, likely the photographs | an afternoon per row, no weekend |
| 6 | The portal spur | second signed creator + one repeating deliverable | two weekends — **deletable, a spur** |
| 7 | Down, or out | Checkpoint C firing at ~20 bookings | the remaining months; near zero in the warm branch |

### New material from T, worked in

> *"we've added a 'careers' tab to the site where people can sign up to work for
> the company."* — T, 2026-08-27, **past tense: it exists.**

Marked throughout as **arriving from T, not through `capture`.** It earned no
track of its own; it landed in four places and changed three things.

- **Phase 1** gained three errand items: **who reads the inbox and within what
  window**; are you hiring and for what; **does the agreement already name who
  may speak as a creator**.
- **Phase 2** gained a **funnel seam**. The two audiences *share* all the
  disclosure furniture — **the verifiability ground is audience-neutral, which is
  why the tab costs an afternoon.** They must **not** share the attribution
  field, the link-tag namespace, the booking calendar, the eligibility standard,
  or the split table. Foxy's answer — a second Calendly event type, role
  pre-filled, no ATS — is the cheap correct one. **Applicant data is worker
  data:** no ID at any application stage, the doxxing threat model extends to
  applicants, short retention.
- **Phase 4** gained **Checkpoint F, the inbox bet** — a sixth checkpoint, added
  deliberately. The tab is an interactive affordance and `0009` §6 governs it
  verbatim: **an unread careers form is Foxy's dead chat input with a person's
  hopes attached**, aimed at the labour pool, in a small industry, where `0009`
  §3's blast-radius argument applies unmodified. Like Checkpoint B it can shrink
  the page: **name a reader and publish the window, or take the tab down** for
  one line and a channel.
- **Phase 5** gained a conditional row: *"a team that answers to you"* becomes
  evidence **the day a role is filled, not the day the tab shipped.**

**The structural change, and the sharpest thing the decomposition produced:**

> **The clause precedes the hire.** A person hired to speak as a creator is
> exactly what `0011` §4.5's disclosure clause is about. Hire a chatter before
> the clause exists and the first creator's agreement is silent about the person
> now typing in her DMs — **and the strongest unclaimed position on the record is
> spent before it was taken.** The Forager's finding 4 is now a **deadline**, not
> an observation.

## What the decomposition exposed that the Trajectory did not know

1. **The notes capture's fuse is already burning.** `0012` put Leg 2 *"the same
   week as launch or the week after."* Wrong about item 1, and wrong by the
   Trajectory's own rule — **the fuse is measured in calls, and he is already
   taking calls.** Phase 3 item 1 **overlaps Phase 2** and does not wait for the
   page. The overlap is named rather than the numbering pretending to be a queue.
2. **The support-channel question belongs in the message.** It was left off
   Leg 0 as "his operational call." That was wrong — it surfaces in Phase 3 as
   *the meter cannot be built.* **Now question 5.**
3. **The claims list is free during Phase 2 and expensive afterwards.** Keeping a
   plain list while the page is being written costs zero minutes. **Available
   only during Phase 2.**
4. **The careers tab is the one fuse on the route already lit, at zero build
   cost** — and nobody was watching it. It produces facts (who applies, for which
   roles, from where) with **no habit required**, so under the watch/ask
   distinction it survives Checkpoint A — **which also means it cannot hide
   behind a failed one.**
5. **The likeliest drift at Checkpoint E is now the careers tab** — a role that
   closed in month two, on a surface nobody on the creator funnel ever visits,
   **which is exactly the condition under which things rot.**
