# State 0007

## Established

T dispatched `challenge` at the six-month picture before anything is built on
it. **There was no Horizon to attack** — `envision` has not run, and the phrase
T recalled ("specificity substitutes for proof") is nowhere on the record. The
attack went to the artifact the Horizon would have been built from: The
Gardener's Framing, whose load-bearing claim is the same shape.

The Advocate ran, and the claim broke.

### The verdict

**The central claim splits in half.** *Starvu cannot make performance claims*
survives untouched. *There is a proof floor, therefore the site must substitute
**confidence** for a track record* does not.

- **The floor is one hardcoded string in a competitor's JavaScript bundle.**
  AT's "4+ Years / 12+ Creators" was never rendered — it was read out of
  `at-agency.co/assets/index-CHlFk0pP.js`, from the same bundle read that
  produced the $38k/$82k/$124k testimonial figures the Framing lists as
  manufactured. Adjacent constants in one file. The Framing used **modesty as a
  proxy for veracity**. Findings `0003` had already warned that none of these
  numbers evidence anything.
- **"Floor" implies a distribution; this is n=3, and none of the three is a new
  agency.** The sample contains zero instances of the case being framed.
- **The brevity argument is a funnel-position error.** Foxy's `/contact` is
  short because seventeen sections, ~115 posts and eight press outlets sit
  upstream. Its economy is *inherited*; Starvu's page is the whole funnel.
  **The Framing's own no-stubs constraint condemns a page modelled on it** —
  two of state 0006's five gold nuggets contradict each other.
- **"An audience equipped to check" is asserted, and on this record checking
  demonstrably failed.** The Factor, with a browser and five passes, could not
  tell whether Foxy's stock reads as generated. Meanwhile the two references
  that manufacture proof are the two working businesses. Read straight, the
  reference set shows proof furniture *works* and nobody checks.

### The replacement ground

**Verifiability substitutes for a track record — not confidence.** The Advocate
tabled the proof inventory the Framing never counted: on every axis a creator
can independently check — named legal entity, jurisdiction, named answerable
human, 18+/ID verification, per-partner verification, earnings-not-typical,
Fenix non-affiliation, deferred written agreement — **Starvu beats all three
references**, and Eros publishes none of it. Confidence becomes an *output* of
that surface, not an input. Buildable today, honest, differentiating, and
checkable by T.

The stronger argument for the same restraint the Framing wanted does not
require the creator to be a detective: asymmetric blast radius, FTC/PA earnings
exposure, and the friend having to live behind every claim on the call.

## Decisions
