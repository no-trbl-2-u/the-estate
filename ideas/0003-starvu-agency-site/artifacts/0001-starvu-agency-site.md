# The Starvu agency site

## The thought, as it arrived

T is building a website for a friend. The friend is starting a business
handling promotion for models — "onlyfans models, etc." The site is the
first thing being built for it.

T opened the record this way:

> Alright, time to start our first idea/plan to test everything out.
>
> I'm currently building a website for a friend of mine. He's starting a
> business where he's handling promotion for models, onlyfans models, etc.
> Right now, here's where to start:

And then handed over the friend's own words rather than paraphrasing them.

## The friend's note, in his voice

The friend's note arrived as a note — flagged as an aside, "just fyi," which
is how the most load-bearing constraints usually arrive:

> Another note just fyi
>
> We don't want to go super dark, neon lights and sleazy
>
> We want to feel "✨expensive✨" as my partner says

Three things are worth leaving exactly as they are.

**"not super dark, neon lights and sleazy"** is a negative brief. It names a
genre the friend already knows exists and is steering away from — the default
visual register for this industry — without naming what replaces it.

**"✨expensive✨"** is not the word *luxury*, *premium*, or *high-end*. It is
the word *expensive*, wrapped in sparkle emoji. The sparkles are part of the
word. They do something the word alone does not: they hold the aspiration and
a small wink at the aspiration at the same time.

**"as my partner says"** is attribution. The word is not the friend's own — he
is relaying it, and it survived the relay intact enough that he passed it on
verbatim. There is a third voice in this brief, one step further out.

## What came with it

Two images, attached to the same message: a recruitment flyer for **Starvu
LLC** (talent management, OnlyFans & premium content, Pennsylvania) and the
Starvu logo. T asked that they be kept:

> Also, we have a few images he provided (so save these as assets with the
> idea so we can provide them in the final seed).

They are catalogued in `assets/MANIFEST.md`. The binaries are not on disk —
they arrived as conversation attachments — so the manifest's prose stands in
for them.

What the manifest records: near-black navy field, metallic gold, silver/white
chrome, a single royal-blue accent bar. Heavy italic condensed display type.
A five-point star burst around the wordmark, star dotting the "V". "NOW
SIGNING SOLO CREATORS & COUPLES." A monthly gross earning range of
"$15K–$30K." Four benefit cards, a 90-day trial band, an apply block with a
QR placeholder, and a legal disclaimer footer.

And the voice already in the material, operator-to-talent:

> You make the content. We run the business around it.

That sentence is doing a lot. It is a division of labor and a promise in nine
words, and it is already written — not something to be invented.

The flyer also already carries compliance furniture: 18+/ID verification,
both partners in a couple verifying individually, earnings-not-typical
language, an explicit non-affiliation statement with OnlyFans/Fenix, and terms
deferred to a Talent Management Agreement. Someone thought about this before
the website existed.

## Three references, held

T supplied three URLs as the type of site they are trying to build:

> Hold on to these as well. Here are some reference URLs for the type of site
> they are trying to build:
> 1) https://foxy-studios.com/
> 2) https://www.agency-eros.com/?utm_source=1
> 3) https://at-agency.co/?utm_source=feedspot

They are held, not fetched. Nobody has looked at them yet. What "the type of
site they are trying to build" means is therefore still entirely in T's and
the friend's heads and not in the record.

## The tension, left standing

The record already names it and I am not going to resolve it: the supplied
brand and the stated feeling pull apart. The flyer and logo are high-contrast
black-and-gold, star-burst, sports-broadcast — loud. The brief says *not*
dark, not neon, not sleazy; ✨expensive✨. Either the site inherits the
existing mark and softens around it, or the site sets a new visual register
and the mark becomes the odd element in it. Not yet chosen, and it may be that
the friend has not noticed the pull yet.

## The energy of it

Two more things in T's framing that are easy to lose and worth keeping.

"time to start our first idea/plan to test everything out" — this record is
doing double duty. It is a real site for a real friend with a real business,
and it is also the first live exercise of this system. Both are true and both
carry weight.

"I'll let you decide where to start with this idea" — T handed over the
starting point rather than setting it. That is a deliberate release of the
first move.
