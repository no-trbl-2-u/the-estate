# The North Star

## What T declared

This arrived after the first Spark and on the same record. T named it himself,
and named it as the thing the whole idea points at:

> Hold this in the record as well. Here's the North Star:

Then the declaration, in full, exactly as written:

> """The goal is for adult film creators to visit the site and feel confident
> that "Starvu" can advise them and support them in growing their adult film
> platform platform accounts. What I want them to do on the site is to learn
> more about our services like chatting, advising, content protection, etc. but
> ultimately want them to book a time on the calendar for 1:1 consultation"""

The doubled word — "adult film platform platform accounts" — is in the
original. It is left as written. It is the kind of thing that happens when a
sentence is composed at speed, and the speed is part of the record.

And the sentence after it, which is not part of the North Star but tells us
what the North Star is *for*:

> Once you hold that as the "North Star" of this entire idea, we can discuss
> next steps

So this is being placed deliberately, before the next move, as the fixed point
the next move gets measured against.

## What is in the words

Four things arrived here that were not in the record before, and each one is
worth leaving in T's own vocabulary rather than mine.

**"adult film creators."** Not "models," not "OnlyFans models, etc.," which is
how the first Spark had it from T's opening and from the flyer. A different
phrase for the same population — more specific about the work, less tied to one
platform. Both phrasings now sit in the record. I am not going to reconcile
them.

**"feel confident."** The first thing the site has to do is not to inform and
not to convert — it is to produce a feeling, and the feeling is *confidence in
Starvu's competence*. Note that this is a second feeling-word in a record that
already has one. The friend supplied ✨expensive✨. T supplies *confident*.
They are not the same word and they are not obviously the same target.

**The services, named for the first time:** "chatting, advising, content
protection, etc." Three named and an explicit "etc." — the list is open. Until
now the record held only the flyer's four unnamed benefit cards and the nine
words "You make the content. We run the business around it." This is the first
time anyone has said what Starvu actually *does*, in T's own enumeration.

**"book a time on the calendar for 1:1 consultation."** A single named
conversion action, marked "ultimately." Learning about services is the middle
of the path; the booking is the end of it. This is the first time the record has
contained anything a visitor is supposed to *do*.

## What it settles

It settles what the site is *for*, which nothing in the record settled before.
Everything held so far was material — a mark, a flyer, a palette, a negative
brief, three unfetched URLs. This is the first statement of purpose, and T has
named it the North Star of the entire idea, not of a page or a section.

The path it draws is short and ordered: **arrive → feel confident → learn the
services → book the 1:1.** Four beats, and the last one is flagged as the
ultimate one.

## What it touches without settling

Two things the record was already carrying get pressed on here. Neither
resolves, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.

**Whose site this is.** The record's open question asked whether this is a
recruitment site (talent applying) or a clientele site — the flyer being pure
recruitment. T has now answered something about audience: the visitor is an
adult film creator, and the creator is the one being advised, supported, and
booked. That is the same population the flyer recruits. But "recruitment" and
"book a 1:1 consultation" are not the same funnel, and a consultation is not a
signing. The audience is now named; whether the site's job is to recruit them,
sell them a service, or something that is both, is not.

**The mark against the feeling.** The first Spark left a clean fracture
standing: the black-and-gold star-burst sports-broadcast mark against "not
dark, not neon, not sleazy; ✨expensive✨." The North Star adds weight to that
seam rather than releasing it. A site with a named conversion action has to
carry a visitor across four beats without losing them, and "feel confident" is
now a stated requirement rather than an aesthetic preference — so the visual
register has a job to do and a way to fail. New pressure on the same fracture.
Still not chosen.

## What is still only T's

One thing to keep visible: this is T's declaration, in T's voice — "What **I**
want them to do on the site." The friend's voice supplied the negative brief and
✨expensive✨; the partner's voice supplied the word itself. The North Star is
the first thing on this record that comes from T and not through him. Whether
the friend whose business this is would state the same North Star has not been
asked.
