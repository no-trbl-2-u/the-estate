# Phase 1 — The errand

I am The Surveyor. This is my own route decomposed, and I begin where I ended:
with the cheapest thing on it, which gates the most.

Nothing in this Phase is a build. Every item is answerable by one person in one
sitting, and every item gates something months long. It is first not because it
is easy but because **everything downstream of it is either unblocked by it or
made cheaper by it**, and because two of its items expire.

---

## What this Phase delivers

Nine written answers, in one file, dated. That is the whole artifact of this
Phase and it is deliberately unglamorous.

### The friend's, in one message

| # | Question | Gates |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Will you publish the Talent Management Agreement?** | Day-one features 1–2, the clause finder, portal item 1 — the Horizon's entire spine |
| 2 | **Will your name and face go on the page?** | The identity block; the verifiability ground itself |
| 3 | **What is the split?** | The split table, which replaced the refused calculator |
| 4 | **Do you actually perform content protection today — and if so, how does a creator's leak reach you right now?** | The takedown assistant (Phase 4), and `0009`'s live doubt on assumption 3 |
| 5 | **Where does support live, and can that channel export?** | The response-time meter's fuse (Phase 3). His operational call, not a build |

Question 5 was named in the Trajectory as the meter's gate and left off the
message because it is an operational decision rather than a question. Decomposing
changed my mind about that. **A gate that nobody asks about is not a gate, it is
a surprise**, and this one surfaces in Phase 3 as *the meter cannot be built* if
the answer is "three apps and my head." It costs one more line in a message that
is being sent anyway, which is the same argument that put question 4 on it.

### The careers items — arriving from T, not through `capture`

**T reported on 2026-08-27 that the site now has a careers tab where people can
sign up to work for the company. Past tense. It exists.** This has not been
through `capture` and is not an artifact finding; I take it as given and work it
in where it falls. It falls here first, and it falls harder here than anywhere
else on the route.

| # | Question | Whose | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Who reads the careers inbox, and within what window?** | the friend | Whether the tab ships in v1 at all (Phase 2), and Checkpoint F (Phase 4) |
| 7 | **Are you hiring right now, and for which roles?** | the friend | Whether the tab is a fact or a claim; its row in the claims ledger (Phase 3) |
| 8 | **Does the agreement name who may speak as a creator, and what they may not say?** | the friend + whoever drafted the TMA | The chatter hire; the strongest unclaimed disclosure position on the record (`0011` §4.5) |

**Why 6 is not a nicety.** `0009` §6 gave the no-stub rule its final wording:
*no interactive affordance may promise a capability that does not exist behind
it — a form must go somewhere a human reads.* A careers form nobody reads is
Foxy's dead chat input with a person's hopes attached to it, and it is aimed at
exactly the labour pool this business will need. The tab does not need an ATS.
Foxy runs its entire hiring funnel on a second Calendly event type with the role
pre-filled and no form at all (`0006`); Eros runs a six-field form to a Telegram
handle. Both are cheap. **Neither is unread.**

**Why 8 has changed character.** It was, in `0011`, an opportunity — a clause
that costs a paragraph and converts into *"our agreement names exactly who can
speak as you, and what they cannot say,"* a sentence no competitor can say about
a practice all of them perform. The careers tab converts it from an opportunity
into a **sequencing constraint with a deadline**. A person hired to speak as a
creator is precisely what that clause is about. Hire the chatter before the
clause exists and the first creator's agreement is silent about the person now
typing in her DMs — and the record's best unclaimed position is spent before it
was ever taken. **The clause must precede the hire.** That ordering is free if
noticed now and unrecoverable if noticed after.

### T's two, today

- **Choose the verification vendor, and stop identity documents arriving over
  WhatsApp.** This gates nothing downstream and belongs at the front anyway. It
  is the only item on the entire route where delay *compounds*: every week it
  waits is another week of photographs of driver's licences accumulating in a
  phone's cloud backup, outside anything T builds and past any retention policy
  he could later write. Everything else here gets cheaper or stays flat if
  deferred. This one gets more expensive, silently, and the cost is somebody
  else's safety.
- **Write down N.** The number of bookings, out of the first twenty non-referral
  visitors, below which the page did not do the work. Any N. **A number
  pre-registered after launch is not a pre-registration** — this is the only
  falsifier on the record that expires, and it expires on the day Phase 2 ships.

## What starts it

Nothing. That is the property that makes it first.

It does not wait on the v1 design, which is in flight. It does not wait on The
Chancellor. It does not wait on a build. **Monday morning, before coffee, from a
phone.**

## What it is waiting on

Only a reply, and only after the message is sent. The vendor decision and N wait
on nobody at all.

## Cost

**An afternoon**, at the outside, and most of that afternoon is waiting.

## Done looks like

- One file in the repo with nine dated answers in it. Not a memory of a
  conversation.
- A verification vendor chosen, and the ID intake changed.
- **N written down, dated, before the page is live.**
- If any answer is *no*, the *no* is written down too. A refusal recorded is
  worth as much here as a yes, and more than an absence.

---

## The checkpoint that can fire here

There is no scheduled checkpoint in this Phase. There is something better: the
record's **friend-publishes bet** (`0010` falsifier 6) can fire on day one,
before anything has been built, which is exactly where you want a falsifier to
fire.

**If the answer to question 1 is no:** features 1 and 2 do not ship, the clause
finder leaves the route entirely, portal item 1 empties out, and the Horizon
loses its spine. **The re-route is not to build the page anyway.** It is to
return to `envision` with a much harder problem, because the verifiability
ground was funded by publication. Eros chose exactly this and calls it
discretion; that is a legitimate business posture and it is a different Horizon.

**If the answer to question 4 is no** — he does not perform content protection —
the takedown assistant leaves Phase 4, Checkpoint B never runs, and the services
block on the page loses a row before it is written. Cheaper to learn now than
after a weekend.

**If the answer to question 6 is "nobody, really"** — the careers tab does not
ship in Phase 2 in its current form. See Phase 2 for what replaces it.

## Where a standing decision changes this Phase

**None of the three bite here, and the portal tension does not either.** That is
worth stating rather than leaving silent: this Phase can be executed in full
while every open decision on the record remains open. It is the only Phase of
which that is true without qualification.

## What runs beside this Phase, on no Phase

**`research` on platform and model-provider terms** — the only genuinely
checkable unknown the Forager opened (`0011` §4.4, §4.6). It is not on the
critical path of anything in Phases 1 through 7, because every tool on this
route handles *business* text. It is on the critical path of the chat-drafting
assistant, which is off the route entirely and stays off it until that research
returns. It can run at any time and should run before a line of that code is
written. **The careers tab does not change that** — hiring a chatter is a
staffing question, not a model-provider question; it is the *tool* that is
gated, not the person.

---

## What this Phase is not

- **Not a dispatch.** `0009` said it, the Trajectory carried it, and I carry it
  again: this is one message and two decisions. Nobody should spawn anything.
- **Not a negotiation.** Questions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 are the friend's business
  calls. This Phase records his answers; it does not lobby for them. Whether he
  publishes his name, publishes the agreement, sets the split at any particular
  number, or performs content protection is his to decide and not this record's.
- **Not blocked by the careers tab.** The tab is live. Nothing in this Phase asks
  T to take it down; three of its items ask what is behind it.
