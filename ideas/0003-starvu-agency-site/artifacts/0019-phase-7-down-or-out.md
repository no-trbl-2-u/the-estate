# Phase 7 — Down, or out

The last Phase, and the only one whose content is genuinely unknown today.

**The page is the same page in both branches.** Every day-one feature is correct
for a warm visitor and for a cold one — a creator arriving from a DM is checking
that the person who messaged her is real and will not embarrass her, and a
creator arriving cold is evaluating an unknown vendor. Disclosure answers both.
That is the useful property of building out of facts: it is temperature-neutral,
and it is why this fork was safe to carry unresolved through five Phases.

---

## CHECKPOINT C — roughly twenty bookings. The traffic read.

This checkpoint is the trigger for this Phase, so it is documented here rather
than at the end of the previous one.

*The "How did you hear about Starvu?" answers settle warm versus cold.*

**It fires on booking volume, not on calendar.** It may therefore arrive before or
after Phase 5's harvest completes, and the plan tolerates that; nothing in Phase
5 waits for it and it waits for nothing in Phase 5.

**If Phase 2's fifteen minutes were cut, this checkpoint cannot be read.** Not
"is harder to read" — cannot. There is no denominator, the per-channel tags do
not exist, and the field is either absent or shared with a second population. The
honest response is to record the fork as **permanently unsettled** and to say so
on the record, rather than to read a field that cannot support the reading. That
is the `unfalsifiable-by-construction` failure `0009` §4 diagnosed, arriving on
schedule.

**Arriving from T, and it is why the funnel seam in Phase 2 was written as hard
as it was:** the careers tab means *how did you hear about Starvu?* now has two
possible populations who could answer it. If the applicant path writes into the
same field, this checkpoint is reading a mixture, and a mixture cannot be
un-mixed after the fact. The separation costs nothing in Phase 2 and cannot be
bought back here.

## What this Phase delivers

### If warm — the page goes down, not out

The friend DMs the link. This is the only distribution mechanism in evidence
anywhere in Starvu's own material, and it is the branch the record should expect
rather than hope against.

The page's job is corroboration, so the remaining months go **deeper**:

- Depth on the agreement — more of it exposed, better navigated, the clause
  finder's failure log feeding what gets clarified.
- Depth on the ninety days — the schedule drawn more precisely from real
  onboardings that have now happened.
- Depth on the post-booking experience — the confirmation, the WhatsApp thread,
  what the first week actually looks like.
- Photographs sit **mid-page as reinforcement** rather than above the fold.

**No acquisition surface is built at all.** No blog, no lead magnet, no referral
programme.

And the honest possibility, which is a finding rather than a failure: **the page
may never have been the load-bearing instrument.** `0009` named that as the
disconfirming observation and `0010` carried it. If the friend closes creators
over WhatsApp without them meaningfully reading the site, that is worth knowing,
and it is worth more than six months of page work aimed at the wrong organ.

### If cold — one instrument, and only one

Photographs and Starvu's own numbers move **above the fold** and become
load-bearing, because a stranger needs a reason to stay before she will read
anything.

And the six months buy **exactly one acquisition instrument: a referral mechanism
with real attribution.** It is AT's, it is the cheapest in the reference set, and
it becomes honest for Starvu specifically once there are signed creators to do
the referring — which Phases 4 and 5 will have established.

**Not a blog.** ~115 posts is a different business with a different staff, and
the record's own evidence on cold acquisition is that it costs either that or
paid traffic plus attribution. That is the far horizon and it is expensive.

## What starts it

**Checkpoint C resolving.** Nothing else.

## What it is waiting on

The bookings. Which is to say: the friend's operating, not T's building.

## Cost

**The remaining months** — and in the warm branch, deliberately close to zero in
build terms. A branch whose correct answer is *build no acquisition surface* is a
cheap branch, and that is a feature of having asked the question rather than
assumed it.

## Done looks like

- One branch chosen, in writing, with the field's actual answers recorded next to
  it — not a recollection of what the answers felt like.
- **N compared against what actually happened.** The pre-registered number from
  Phase 1 exists to be read here, and reading it is the only thing that converts
  the bet into a claim.
- The other branch's work explicitly not done, and recorded as not done.

## CHECKPOINT E — month six. The no-stub audit.

*Walk the page. If any affordance has been quietly disabled, unlinked, or drifted
out of true, the discipline failed.*

A slot that is not real. A service he stopped offering. A response-time number
that is now last quarter's. **A careers tab advertising a role that closed in
month two** — and that one is the likeliest drift on the whole page, because
nobody on the creator funnel ever visits it, which is exactly the condition under
which things rot.

**If the claims ledger shipped in Phase 3, this is not a walk.** It is a build
step that has been failing loudly since the day the drift started, and the
checkpoint is a formality. That is the conversion worth having: **a habit becomes
a mechanism.**

**If the ledger did not ship, this is ten minutes of walking and it will be
skipped**, because month-six habits set in month zero are the ones that do not
survive. Say so plainly now rather than discover it then.

## Where a standing decision changes this Phase

**Decision 2 bites here in an indirect way that has not been weighed against it.**
A gated calendar lengthens the fuse on Checkpoint C — fewer bookings per visitor,
so twenty bookings take longer, so this Phase starts later — and it biases the
answers toward people willing to clear a gate, which is a different population
from the one the branch is about. **That is a route-grounds argument for *bare*,
offered to The Chancellor as input and not taken.**

**Decisions 1 and 3** are spent by now; their only residue is the disclaimer row
in the ledger, which Checkpoint E re-verifies like everything else.

**The portal tension** does not reach this Phase. Phase 6 is a spur and this
Phase does not link to it in either branch.

## What this Phase is not

- **Not a redesign at month six.** The page that arrives here is the page that
  shipped in Phase 2 with true things added to it. If it needs redesigning, that
  is Checkpoint D's business, not this Phase's, and Checkpoint D sends the record
  back to `envision` rather than forward to a rebuild.
- **Not both branches.** Building depth *and* an acquisition instrument is what
  the checkpoint exists to prevent, and it is what will feel reasonable on the
  day.
- **Not the end of the record.** After this the record is `seed`-shaped:
  Horizon + Trajectory + Phases is the terminal export, and everything here is
  most of it.
