# frame — `Spark → Framing`

From the Spark, state: the problem or question underneath it, who it is for,
what is in and out of scope, and the tension that makes it interesting. A good
Framing lets a stranger work on the idea without meeting the operator.

Do not solve, do not envision, do not plan — naming the space is the whole job.

Produce one `type: Framing` artifact (frontmatter per `templates/artifact.md`,
`inputs:` pointing at the Spark) with `potential-next-steps` (usually
`[envision, explore, challenge]`). Close per protocol.

## Agent binding (hard)

Performed by **The Gardener** (`gardener`) and by no one else. If that agent is
unavailable the verb does not run; the Steward reports the gap.
