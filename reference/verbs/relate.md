# relate

|---|---|
| Signature | `(Idea, Idea) → relates` |
| Family | edge author |
| Performed by | [The Cartographer](../offices/cartographer.md) — a [hard binding](../law.md) |

Draw the one **hand-authored edge** in the system: this idea relates to
that idea. Every other lineage relation is derived from composition
chains; `relates` comes out of a head, and it is the connection the
machinery cannot make.

The work splits along the [writer seam](../law.md): the Cartographer names
the edge and, above all, **why** — the why is the payload — and the
Steward writes it (record frontmatter is state, and state has one writer).
The only verb in the edge-author family, and it will stay that way.
