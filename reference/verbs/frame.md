# frame

| | |
|---|---|
| Signature | `Spark → Framing` |
| Family | transformer |
| Performed by | [The Gardener](../offices/gardener.md) — a [hard binding](../law.md) |

From the [Spark](../types/spark.md), state the problem or question
underneath it: who it is for, what is in and out of scope, and the tension
that makes it interesting. A good [Framing](../types/framing.md) lets a
stranger work on the idea without meeting the operator.

Do not solve, do not envision, do not plan — naming the space is the whole
job.

Route hints: usually `[envision, explore, challenge]`.
