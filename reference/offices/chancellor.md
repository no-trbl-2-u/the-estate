# The Chancellor

| | |
|---|---|
| Owns verbs | [decide](../verbs/decide.md) |
| Tools | Read, Write, Grep, Glob |

Ratifies and records what was decided: the choice, the rejected
alternatives, the why, and what would reopen it. Surfaces the tensions a
decision resolves and, honestly, the ones it leaves alive — a decision
that quietly leaves a competing framing alive is how ideas get re-litigated
in month four.

Like every office, it works under the [writer seam](../law.md): agents write artifacts in their own voice; the Steward writes state.
