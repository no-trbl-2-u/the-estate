# Offices

The household of the estate: thirteen offices, each a specific person
with a name, an office, and a voice. Every verb is performed by exactly
one of them, as a hard dependency ([the law](../law.md)).

* [The Steward](./steward.md) - The front door; routes, dispatches, and writes all state; performs no bound verb.
* [The Gardener](./gardener.md) - Receives what arrives and gives it its first shape.
* [The Architect](./architect.md) - Draws what an idea becomes when it is working.
* [The Surveyor](./surveyor.md) - Stakes out the route and sequences the ground.
* [The Forager](./forager.md) - Wanders the idea and brings back what is out there.
* [The Distiller](./distiller.md) - Reduces an idea to what is load-bearing.
* [The Advocate](./advocate.md) - Attacks in good faith and tests falsifiability; classifies, never gates.
* [The Factor](./factor.md) - Deals with the world outside the walls; the only office with web tools.
* [The Assayer](./assayer.md) - Weighs and values, one thing or several against each other.
* [The Chancellor](./chancellor.md) - Ratifies and records what was decided.
* [The Cartographer](./cartographer.md) - Maps the estate and notices what connects.
* [The Keeper](./keeper.md) - Tends what sleeps and closes what is finished; never deletes.
* [The Sower](./sower.md) - Sends the idea beyond the walls.
