# Artifact Types

Ten typed values. The verb fixes an artifact's *type* (composition and
gap-derivation need it); the operator picks its *shape*
([shapes](../shapes.md)). `Text` and `Question` are boundary inputs -
operator words crossing into the system, not artifacts in it - and
`Tensions` lives in the head state snapshot, whose path is a legal
`inputs:` target.

* [Spark](./spark.md) - A raw capture: minimal processing, maximal fidelity to the original thought.
* [Framing](./framing.md) - The problem, question, or opportunity, stated.
* [Horizon](./horizon.md) - The elaborated six-month vision: what the idea looks like when it is working.
* [Trajectory](./trajectory.md) - The rough path from here to the Horizon, terminating in something startable.
* [Phase](./phase.md) - One sequenced step of a Trajectory; recursive.
* [Findings](./findings.md) - Gathered information with its sources, honestly bounded by what was not found.
* [Appraisal](./appraisal.md) - A judgment of one thing, or of several against each other.
* [Decision](./decision.md) - An explicit recorded choice: decided, rejected, why, and what would reopen it.
* [SessionResidue](./session-residue.md) - What a session revealed about the estate's own functioning, observed while doing something else.
* [Brief](./brief.md) - The early-exit export: a run stopped before Seed-shape, exported anyway.
* [Seed](./seed.md) - The terminal export: horizon plus trajectory, thin on implementation, clean.

Boundary inputs are not artifact types: `Text` (capture), `Question`
(research), `Direction` (graft), and `Slip` — the one that is durable,
written to `inbox/` by clerical `jot` and consumed later
by `capture`.

# Verb families

* Refiner `a → a` - explore, distill, challenge
* Transformer `a → b` - capture, frame, envision, chart, research, review, decide, seed, graft
* Decomposer `a → [b]` - phase
* Aggregator `[a] → b` - compare
* Reader `[Idea] → r` - survey
* Transition `Idea → Idea` - incubate, retire
* Edge author `(Idea, Idea) → relates` - relate
